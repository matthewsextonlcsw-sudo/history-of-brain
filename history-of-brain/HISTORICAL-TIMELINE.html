A timeline without a trapdoor

History did not march everywhere through the same ancient, medieval, and modern stages. These overlapping bands help readers travel; every page still has to name the place and dates where its story applies.

Equal space does not mean equal duration. Deep prehistory would swallow the screen on a mathematically scaled line. Here, every band gets enough room to explore—and its date label tells the truth.
