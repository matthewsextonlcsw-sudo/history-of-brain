Every packet gets a contract

Question

One evidence problem in one sentence.

Dates

Start, end, local rationale, and exceptions.

Places

Named geography; never a continent treated as one culture.

In / out

Frozen scope plus topics owned by neighboring packets.

Evidence streams

Primary, scholarly, lived, family, workers, institutions, absences.

Terms

Period vocabulary, languages, translations, modern search bridges.

Claims to test

Myths, “firsts,” diagnoses, disagreements, treatment claims.

Connections

Named routes and mechanisms—not resemblance alone.

Current evidence

Historical use and present effectiveness stay separate.

Stop condition

Files and checks complete; no public prose begins.

Done means checked

Packet gate

  • Consequential claims point to opened sources.
  • Primary evidence and interpretation are distinct.
  • Direct voice, mediated voice, and reconstruction are distinct.
  • Archive and language limits are visible.
  • Geography and dates stayed inside the brief.
  • A different researcher completed cross-review.

Wave gate

  • Conflicting dates and interpretations are recorded.
  • Shared citation chains are detected.
  • Missing regions, voices, and kinds of care are identified.
  • Unsupported exchange claims are removed.
  • Duplicate page ideas are consolidated.
  • The next wave receives explicit inherited questions.

Start with six narrow packets

The safest calibration batch is R01, R02, R03-Egypt, R03-Nubia-Kush, R04, and R05-Greek. R05-Roman and R06 wait for the next batch. This tests archaeology without mind-reading, early texts without retrospective diagnosis, uneven archives, multiple types of care, and several local chronologies.

After those six packets, we improve the template before opening the next batch. We do not create 42 empty folders, and we do not draft public articles yet.
