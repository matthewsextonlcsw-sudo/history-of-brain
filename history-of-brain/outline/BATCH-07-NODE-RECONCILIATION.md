## Cross-node rules

1. Self-writing, published transcription, literary reworking, committee evidence, administrative description, catalogue metadata, and scholar paraphrase are separate evidence layers.
2. A person's words are direct for what that person wrote, subject to the surviving object and mediation chain. They do not automatically prove event truth, diagnosis, cause, consent, efficacy, representativeness, or outcome.
3. Shared objects and derivative publications do not become independent corroboration: diary and fiction share one authorial-development chain; the Wilbraham transcription and article share one publication chain; Seacliff's two articles share a scholarly chain; Packard's selected contributors remain inside one edited publication chain.
4. A statute, committee report, institutional record, family trace, archive catalogue, later scholarship, and project acquisition audit perform different jobs and cannot corroborate one another merely by coexisting.
5. A record of committal, discharge, transfer, work, revenue, payment, letter retention, or administrative routing does not establish consent, recovery, coercion, therapy, motive, benefit, harm, or implementation unless separately evidenced.
6. Absence statements stay bounded to the complete object or search actually audited. They do not prove that family, worker, Black-patient, isiXhosa, Māori, Malay/Jawi, community, or affected-person evidence never existed.
7. Historical and current place names, colonial jurisdictions, language witnesses, archive power, and present community authority must remain visible. One named case never upgrades national, regional, racialized, Indigenous, or institutional coverage by proxy.
8. Repository visibility, public-domain status, open licensing, research access, copying, quotation, facsimile reuse, commercial publication, privacy, dignity, descendant/community authority, Indigenous data sovereignty, and public indexing are separate gates.
9. No medical portrait, signature, handwriting decoration, clinical roster, family graph, sensational title, intimate allegation, long quotation, or searchable person profile is authorized by this reconciliation.
10. None of these placements authorizes public prose, SEO/GEO claims, timeline copy, blog copy, video scripts, reels, images, translations, implementation, ingestion, or publication.
