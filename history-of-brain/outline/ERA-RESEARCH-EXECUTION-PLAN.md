# Era Research Execution Plan

**Status:** Proposed execution architecture; ready for Matthew's review

**Last updated:** Aug. 18, 2026

**Authority:** `../PROJECT-BRAIN.md`, `CHRONOLOGICAL-BACKBONE.md`, and
`../sources/EVIDENCE-MODEL.md`

## The ruling

The eleven public timeline bands are **reader navigation**, not research assignments. No
researcher receives “cover ancient history,” “cover the modern era,” or “research the history
of mental health.” Those scopes are too large to investigate carefully and invite geographic
drift, retrospective diagnosis, weak citations, and accidental omission.

The research unit is an **Era Research Packet**: one bounded period or historical change in
one named regional and evidentiary context, with required evidence streams, explicit
exclusions, defined neighbors, and a stop condition. Research comes before public writing.
The 42 sections below are a planning map, not automatically 42 runnable prompts. Any section
that still spans several regions, languages, or archives must split into suffixed packets such
as `R14-WA` and `R14-EA` before a session starts.

> **Research first. Reconcile the seams second. Write third.**

## Two chronological layers

| Layer | Purpose | Size | State |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Public bands `T00–T10` | Help readers travel through a global history | 11 broad, overlapping bands | Mapped |
| Research sections `R01–R42` | Divide the historical field into smaller evidence problems | 42 section lanes in 7 planning waves | Proposed |
| Runnable packets `Rxx-suffix` | Give one session one date/place/archive problem | As many as responsible scoping requires; batches of 6 maximum | Future, created at brief freeze |

Packets may overlap in dates because change did not happen everywhere at once. Each overlap
must have a reason: exchange, translation, migration, empire, institutional transfer,
resistance, or an evidence handoff. A shared date range alone is not a connection.

## Parallel operating model

One execution batch uses no more than seven active sessions:

- **one coordinator** owns the project brain, packet index, seam audit, and synthesis;
- **up to six researchers** each own one packet folder and do not edit the master truth files;
- after the six packets finish, the coordinator checks conflicts, gaps, and handoffs;
- researchers then rotate into cross-review so nobody is the sole reviewer of their own work;
- the next batch starts only after the coordinator records the prior batch's state exactly.

Parallel work is safe only because file ownership does not overlap. A researcher may alert the
coordinator to a needed master change but may not make it directly.

one-session assignments. Local chronologies override the orienting dates. During brief freeze,
the coordinator must split every multi-region lane until one researcher can responsibly own
its dates, places, languages, archives, and required evidence streams.

## The packet contract

Every research session receives a filled brief with these fields. Blank fields mean the
packet is not ready to start.

1. **Packet ID and question.** One research problem in one sentence.
2. **Date boundary.** Beginning, end, and why those boundaries make sense locally.
3. **Geographic boundary.** Named places; never “Africa,” “Asia,” or “the Indigenous view” as
   if each were a single society.
4. **In scope / out of scope.** Neighboring subjects assigned elsewhere.
5. **Required evidence streams.** Primary/material evidence, scholarship, institutional
   records, lived experience, family/community care, workers, and missing voices.
6. **Terms and languages.** Period words, transliterations, translation disputes, and modern
   search-language bridges without retrospective diagnosis.
7. **Claims to test.** Including likely myths, “first” claims, and credible disagreements.
8. **Connections.** Named people, texts, institutions, technologies, routes, or power
   mechanisms linking this packet to another.
9. **Current-evidence boundary.** Historical use and present effectiveness are researched
   separately.
10. **Deliverables and stop condition.** The session stops when the required files and gate
    checks are complete; it does not begin public prose.

## Packet deliverables

Each runnable packet will eventually own a folder under
`research/era-packets/Rxx-suffix-short-name/` with:

| File | Job |
|---|---|
| `BRIEF.md` | Frozen scope, questions, exclusions, and neighbors |
| `SOURCE-LEDGER.md` | Every opened source, exact support, limits, and stable link |
| `FINDINGS.md` | Evidence-led findings; not public prose |
| `CLAIM-MAP.md` | Candidate claims with evidence labels and source independence |
| `LIVED-EXPERIENCE-AND-ARCHIVE.md` | Direct, mediated, absent, or protocol-limited voices |
| `GAPS-AND-HANDOFF.md` | Unknowns, disputes, overlaps, and messages for adjacent packets |

Do not create folders for all 42 sections. Create a packet folder only when its approved,
properly narrowed brief and real research work begin.

## Research gate

A packet is not complete because it has many links. It passes only when:

- every consequential candidate claim points to an opened and checked source;
- primary evidence and later interpretation are distinguishable;
- direct lived experience is separated from reported speech and reconstruction;
- archive silences and language limitations are explicit;
- contested claims carry the appropriate evidence label;
- “first,” diagnosis, treatment-success, and civilizational-progress shortcuts are tested;
- geography and dates have not drifted beyond the brief;
- adjacent packets received clear handoffs;
- a different researcher completed a source-and-scope cross-review; and
- the coordinator recorded **Sourced** or sent the packet back with exact gaps.

## Batch seam-synthesis gate

After a batch of up to six packets finishes, the coordinator creates one seam synthesis that
answers:

1. Where do dates or interpretations conflict?
2. Which claims rely on the same citation chain rather than independent evidence?
3. Which people appear only through institutions, colonizers, courts, or clinicians?
4. Which region, language, profession, community, or type of care is underrepresented?
5. Which supposed exchange lacks a demonstrated route?
6. Which repeated topic belongs in one packet with links rather than six duplicate summaries?
7. What must the next wave inherit, challenge, or leave unresolved?

No coordinator may hide a contradiction simply to make the timeline read smoothly.

## When writing may begin

Writing does **not** begin after an individual researcher says “done.” A public cluster may
move from **Sourced** to **Drafted** only when:

- its packet passed the research gate;
- its relevant predecessor and successor handoffs were reviewed;
- its batch seam synthesis passed;
- the claim map identifies the page's exact job and avoids duplication; and
- the coordinator assigns a separate drafting session using only the approved packet.

This permits careful pipeline overlap without asking one model session to research, reconcile,
and write an entire era from memory. The writer receives an evidence packet, not the open web
and an invitation to improvise.

## Exact session brief pattern

> You own packet `Rxx` and only its folder. You are not alone in the workspace; do not revert
> or rewrite another session's files. Research the frozen question inside the named dates and
> places. Open and check every cited source. Record evidence and uncertainty; do not write
> public article prose. When a discovery belongs to another packet, put it in the handoff file
> instead of expanding your scope. Stop when the packet deliverables and gate checklist are
> complete.

## Recommended first execution

Use **Wave 1** to choose the first calibration batch. A safe first six would be `R01`, `R02`,
`R03-Egypt`, `R03-Nubia-Kush`, `R04`, and `R05-Greek`; `R05-Roman` and `R06` wait for the next
batch. This deliberately tests archaeology without mind-reading, early texts without
retrospective diagnosis, uneven archives, several kinds of care, and multiple local
chronologies. Adjust the packet template based on what the first six researchers actually
needed. Do not freeze every future brief before that test.

The public timeline remains **Mapped**. This plan does not authorize drafting, Couch Commons
implementation, publishing, or deployment.
