## Cross-node rules

1. Self-writing, dictation, translation, elicited examination, institutional periodical writing,
   and administrative paraphrase are never labeled with the single phrase “patient voice.”
2. A record of labor or revenue does not establish consent, coercion, therapeutic purpose, pay,
   safety, meaning, or outcome.
3. Statutes, reports, official inquiries, authored books, archive policies, and case files are
   different acts of authority and cannot corroborate one another merely by coexisting.
4. Public-domain or open access status does not settle dignity, privacy, quotation, image,
   community, descendant, or commercial-use permission.
5. None of these placements authorizes public prose, SEO/GEO claims, video scripts, reels,
   quotations, images, diagnosis, prevalence, efficacy, or representative claims.
