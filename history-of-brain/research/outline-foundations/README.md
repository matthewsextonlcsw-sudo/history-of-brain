## Shared limitations

- The work was conducted primarily through sources discoverable in English, even when it
  identified works, terms, or collections in other languages.
- Web discoverability is uneven and often mirrors colonial, institutional, and publishing
  power.
- A linked source was checked for its outline job; it may not yet be adequate for every claim
  a future page could make.
- Cultural permission, privacy, rights, and Indigenous knowledge governance require their own
  review even when a source is legally accessible.
- The reader-question note is qualitative; it is not keyword-volume or Search Console data.
- Current evidence on treatment effectiveness must be researched separately from historical
  use, intention, or patient experience.
