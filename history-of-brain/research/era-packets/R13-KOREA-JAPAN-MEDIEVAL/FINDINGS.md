## Canonical evidence-field key

Each `### EVD-021xxx` block is one atomic evidence record. The block labels map to the
project schema as follows: `evidence_type` supplies `evidence_form`; `observation` supplies
`verbatim_or_description`; `language_layer` supplies `translation` and `original_text`
status; `locator` identifies the exact passage/object; `supports` is the `researcher_note`;
`does_not_support` is `limitations`; `rating` records directness/claim fit; and `dependency`
records source-chain context.

Shared fields unless a block overrides them: `context_before_after=the complete named unit or
chapter was opened, with adjacent source attributions and notes`; `collector_or_recorder=the
creator/compiler/institution named in SOURCE-LEDGER.md`; `method=full-source textual replay and
edition/translation-layer comparison`; `representativeness=individual narrative unit or bounded
textual compilation, never a population`; `extracted_by=R13 packet researcher`;
`extracted_on=2026-08-18`; `verification_status=checked against source`. `directness=direct` only
for what a source displays, prescribes, or narrates; represented medieval events are `reported`,
archive absences are `inferred from a defined bounded unit`, and modern interpretation is
`reported specialist interpretation`. `speaker_or_subject` is the named compiler/quoted work and
the persons described in each observation. `sensitivity=clinical/identity/sacred`; add
`trauma/violence` for `EVD-021001`, `EVD-021005`, `EVD-021010`, and `EVD-021012`, and
`gender/power` for `EVD-021004`–`EVD-021005` and `EVD-021020`. No unknown consent, motive,
implementation, or result is supplied.
