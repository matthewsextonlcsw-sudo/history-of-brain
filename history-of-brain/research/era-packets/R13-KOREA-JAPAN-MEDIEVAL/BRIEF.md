# R13-KOREA-JAPAN-MEDIEVAL — Two Bounded Regional Lanes and a Contact Question

**State:** Authorized; research only

**Identifier range:** `021000–021999`

## Research question

What can one bounded Korean evidence lane and one bounded Japanese evidence lane from
approximately 600 to 1500 establish about emotion, mind/heart, illness, troubling conduct,
medicine, ritual, family/community response, and care—and what, if anything, can a named contact
chain demonstrate between them or their neighbors?

## Boundary

This is not a combined “East Asian psychology” packet. Select no more than one primary corpus or
local setting per regional lane plus one tightly evidenced transmission/contact question. Keep
Korean, Classical Chinese, Japanese, Buddhist, court, medical, legal, monastic, shrine, and
household evidence source-specific. If adequate full-source evidence cannot support both lanes,
return a documented gap rather than filling it with modern summaries.

## Required evidence

- critical/authoritative texts, translations, manuscript/object/inscription records, legal or
  household evidence, and archaeology with exact locators;
- Korean and Japanese specialist histories of medicine, emotion, religion, family, law,
  disability, gender, and material/textual transmission;
- original terms and translation disputes without modern diagnosis or English equivalence;
- court/elite/monastic prescription separated from household, local, and recipient experience;
- named people, texts, missions, manuscripts, objects, dates, and routes for contact claims;
- archive-loss, colonial collection, modern national canon, translation, and disciplinary bias;
- affected-person, family, caregiver, worker, gender/status, cost, coercion, consent, and outcome
  audit; and
- audits of timeless Kampo, imported-copy narratives, Buddhist-therapy claims, uniquely humane
  tradition claims, and modern national priority stories.

## Stop

Do not treat Korea and Japan as one culture, use China as the default explanation, or infer
transmission from similarity. Create five outputs and rights-safe ignored cache; no public prose.
