# R03-NUBIA-KUSH — Independent Nile-World Evidence

**State:** Authorized; research only

**Owner:** One research session; this folder only

**Identifier range:** `SRC/EVD/CLM-004000–004999`

## Research question

What can evidence from Nubian and Kushite sites, objects, bodies, texts, and later historical
interpretation responsibly establish about care, disability, personhood, suffering, healing,
and contact—without treating these worlds as an Egyptian appendix?

## Boundary

Named Nubian and Kushite polities, communities, sites, and collections, using their local
chronologies from prehistory through approximately 600 CE. Egyptian, Roman, and other sources
enter only with their outside position and mediation made explicit.

## In scope

- material, bioarchaeological, textual, linguistic, funerary, and settlement evidence;
- evidence of impairment, survival, assistance, healing, specialists, or social position when
  responsibly inferable;
- Meroitic-language and translation limitations;
- relations with Egypt, Red Sea, Mediterranean, and African neighbors through named routes;
- excavation and museum histories that shape what survives; and
- Nubian scholars, heritage authorities, descendant/community perspectives, and repatriation
  or ethical constraints where available.

## Out of scope

- projecting Egyptian texts onto Nubian/Kushite people;
- using burial difference alone to infer mental difference or stigma;
- a survey of all African healing;
- unsupported reconstruction of inner experience; and
- public prose.

## Required evidence targets

- primary excavation/object/site reports and authoritative catalogs;
- specialist Nubian/Kushite archaeology and history;
- methodological work on bioarchaeological inference;
- scholarship or institutional records exposing colonial excavation/collection bias;
- locally grounded or community/heritage-authority sources when available; and
- explicit documentation of what Meroitic and archive limits prevent us from knowing.
