# R23 La Castañeda and Alberto Nicolat — Research Brief

**Status:** Conditional candidate for Batch 06 internal research; public use blocked

**Place and dates:** Manicomio General La Castañeda, Mixcoac, Mexico City; Aug. 20, 1918–1932.
Chalco and Veracruz appear only as provenance nodes.

**Question:** How did Alberto Nicolat Talocín, his family, clinicians, courts, and print publics
negotiate confinement, psychiatric classification, racialized heredity, work, criminal
responsibility, and postrevolutionary nation-making?

## Frozen corpus

1. Alberto Nicolat, *El sueño de un loco realizado: pro-paz* (Veracruz, approximately 1928),
   complete official UNAM scan.
2. AHSSA Fondo Manicomio General, Sección Expedientes Clínicos, caja 97, expediente 25, only after
   item-specific classification, access, reproduction, quotation/publication and exact folio
   conditions are confirmed through the current official AHSSA route.
3. Gregorio Oneto Barenque, *Un loco, un anómalo, ¿puede ser responsable?* (1924), only after the
   complete thesis is acquired and checked.
4. Andrés Ríos Molina's full source-mapping study and official UNAM/AHSSA repository controls.

## Required controls

- Nicolat's deliberate publication is direct affected-person authorship for what he chose to
  publish, not automatic verification of every biographical or institutional event.
- Label every case-file voice as autograph, dictated, transcribed, quoted, reported, or
  clinician-paraphrased. The same parent file and later scholarship are not independent sources.
- His wife's reported interview and possible correspondence are mediated family traces until the
  original documents are inspected.
- One person's tailoring or workshop history cannot prove freely chosen, therapeutic, paid, or
  beneficial institutional labor.
- Preserve Spanish text, exact locators, named translator, and translation uncertainty.
- A generic archival description of his mother as “indígena mexicana” does not identify a Nation,
  community, or language and cannot authorize ancestry-based cultural interpretation.
- Do not cache or publish the 12,296-row patient spreadsheet, case-file photographs, handwriting,
  or private family records. Apply necessity, privacy, dignity, descendant-risk, disability/
  survivor, Mexican historical, rights, and item-specific reproduction review.

## Exclusions and stop conditions

Exclude all-La-Castañeda or Latin American generalization, retrospective diagnosis, treatment
success, biological endorsement of degeneration theory, Indigenous mental-health traditions,
raw datasets, clinical images, sensational labels, and post-1950 history. The packet cannot pass
its internal research gate until the full Oneto thesis, item-specific AHSSA access/publication
conditions, consequential case-file folios, and a second Spanish-language check are obtained. The
current general AHSSA service page and 2023 policy do not answer those item-level questions. If
those conditions fail, preserve the Nicolat publication as a bounded lane and hold every file-
dependent claim. No public prose.
