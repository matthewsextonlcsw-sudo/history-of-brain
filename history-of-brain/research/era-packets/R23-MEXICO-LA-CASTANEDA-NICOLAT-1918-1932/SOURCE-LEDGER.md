## Research-route audit

- Agent Reach v1.5.0 was current. Its doctor reported Jina/Web available and Exa unavailable
  because the Exa MCP route was not configured. Direct UNAM, DOI, journal, catalog, PDF and
  repository routes were used instead. Search snippets were leads only.
- Alberto Nicolat's complete 96-image UNAM scan was opened page by page. Because it contains no
  text layer, a private macOS Vision OCR was used only as a finding aid; every cited passage and
  locator was replayed against the scan image.
- Andrés Ríos Molina's complete 2009 Spanish article was opened from the journal PDF and checked
  against its DOI landing page and issue-specific rights statement.
- The UNAM-IIH `Los casos`, `Los pacientes`, `Breve historia`, thesis-list and project-credit pages
  were opened in full. These pages share one project and are not independent corroboration.
- The complete Gregorio Oneto Barenque thesis was not found in the official site's 72 digitized
  theses, DGB catalog attempts, or open web routes. A citation and page references in Ríos are not
  a substitute for opening it.
- A current official Secretaría de Salud Centro de Documentación Institucional service page and
  its complete 11-page January 2023 AHSSA consultation policy were opened. They establish a
  current contact route and general consultation, confidentiality, personal-data and supervised-
  reproduction rules. They do not establish the Nicolat file's classification, eligibility,
  quotation/publication status, commercial-use conditions or consequential folio access. A
  previously plausible gob.mx route still returned 404 on 2026-08-19. No access control was
  bypassed and no outreach was made.
- The 12,000-plus-row patient spreadsheet, clinical-file images, handwriting and private family
  material were neither opened nor cached.
