## Why the planned sections split this way

The master execution plan's `R09–R12` jobs remain planning sections, not permission to assign
one researcher “the medieval world.” Batch 03 separates:

- translation labor from the doctrines later transmitted;
- philosophical and ethical models from medical classifications and treatments;
- named institutions from household, legal, shrine, and community responses; and
- Buddhist route evidence from a universal history of Buddhism or Asia.

East Asian, medieval South Asian, African, Central Asian, Jewish, Christian, European, and
other regional syntheses not responsibly covered by these packets remain later bounded jobs.
Their absence from Batch 03 must not be narrated as historical absence.

## Shared research rules

The rules in `BATCH-01-INDEX.md`, `../../../sources/EVIDENCE-MODEL.md`, and
`../../../sources/SOURCE-RATING-AND-CAPTURE-PROTOCOL.md` remain active.

- Use `L1–L5/LX` source level and `A–D` claim fit for every consequential source–claim job.
- Open full sources and record exact page, passage, object, manuscript, or section locators.
- Abstracts, snippets, catalogs, and summaries may identify leads but cannot silently support
  body-level historical claims.
- Trace priority, continuity, diagnosis, treatment, translation, and institution claims
  upstream. Record shared citation chains as dependent.
- Distinguish author, translator, compiler, scribe, patron, institution, modern editor, and
  modern interpreter.
- Separate composition, translation, manuscript, edition, and reception dates.
- Do not equate historical terms with current diagnoses or English “mind,” “soul,” “emotion,”
  “therapy,” “hospital,” or “patient” without a passage-specific argument.
- Prescriptions, laws, endowments, buildings, and ideals do not prove implementation, access,
  outcomes, consent, or typical practice.
- Seek patients, recipients, workers, family members, enslaved/dependent people, disabled
  people, women, children, poor people, and non-elite communities. State when the archive
  cannot recover them.
- Name languages, regions, communities, routes, and evidence boundaries. “Islamic psychology,”
  “medieval medicine,” and “Buddhist psychology” are not acceptable unitary categories.
- No public prose, SEO copy, quotation clearance, implementation, or publication.
