## Why these packets belong together

Batch 07 asks what happens when an affected person's writing, petition, letter, memoir, fiction,
or public argument survives inside—or alongside—an institutional, legal, family, editorial, or
colonial archive. The packets do not treat “patient voice” as a single transparent category.
They distinguish autograph manuscript, embedded letter, published testimony, petition,
editorial framing, family action, case note, official report, law, press mediation, and later
scholarship.

Three packets closed with bounded internal research lanes; three closed as acquisition `STOP`
records. R27, R29, and R30 must not manufacture substantive history from catalog records or
unopened primary objects; their useful output is a replayable acquisition map, rights and
language gate, and explicit record of what remains unknown.

## Shared packet contract

Every researcher reads `../../../PROJECT-BRAIN.md`, `../../../sources/EVIDENCE-MODEL.md`,
`../../../sources/SOURCE-RATING-AND-CAPTURE-PROTOCOL.md`, this index, and the owned `BRIEF.md`.
Each packet creates exactly:

1. `SOURCE-LEDGER.md`
2. `FINDINGS.md`
3. `CLAIM-MAP.md`
4. `LIVED-EXPERIENCE-AND-ARCHIVE.md`
5. `GAPS-AND-HANDOFF.md`

Every source, atomic evidence record, claim or research decision, disagreement, and relationship
receives one schema-complete canonical record. Use only the assigned ID range and controlled
vocabularies. One evidence record normally names one source. A manuscript, transcription,
edition, scan, OCR layer, quotation in scholarship, and derivative retelling are one dependency
chain unless genuinely independent evidence is demonstrated.

## Full-source and acquisition rules

- Agent Reach v1.5.0 was current at batch start. Jina Reader was available; Exa was unavailable.
  Record the limitation and use direct archival, institutional, primary, and full specialist
  sources.
- Open and replay the complete relevant object. Catalog descriptions, OCR hits, snippets,
  abstracts, and quotations inside scholarship are leads or bounded metadata—not substitutes.
- For manuscripts and case files, bind every claim to a page, folio, canvas, or image and record
  the object's order, missing leaves, hands, annotations, enclosures, and transformation chain.
- R27, R29, and R30 remain substantive `STOP` unless their exact complete files are lawfully
  acquired and image-replayed. Their catalogues can support only catalogue and acquisition
  facts.
- Cache only lawful public-domain or openly licensed research artifacts in ignored
  `research/source-cache/<packet-id>/` directories. Restricted, unclear-rights, private,
  culturally governed, or sensitive records remain link-only. Record URLs, access dates,
  transformations, bytes, checksums, source binding, rights, and allowed use.

## Voice, dignity, sovereignty, and power rules

- Self-writing is direct for what the person wrote, not automatically for event truth,
  diagnosis, causation, representativeness, or outcome.
- Preserve editorial, scribal, translation, journalistic, familial, institutional, legal, and
  archival mediation. Do not make unequal-power accounts into a flattened “both sides” story.
- Do not infer consent, capacity, voluntariness, coercion, recovery, efficacy, motive, diagnosis,
  or prevalence from silence, status labels, transfer, discharge, work, or publication.
- Minimize names and identifying medical, sexual, family, financial, alleged-crime, and
  self-harm details. No searchable clinical roster, sensational quotation, medical portrait,
  signature, family profiling, or diagnosis card.
- Repository access, copyright, image reuse, quotation, privacy, dignity, descendant authority,
  Indigenous data sovereignty, commercial use, and public indexing are separate gates.
- Require the packet-specific language, regional, archive, disability/survivor, privacy,
  descendant/family, community, and human editorial reviews named in each brief.

## Exclusions

No packet becomes a national or continental history of psychiatry. No retrospective diagnosis,
simple humane-versus-brutal binary, institutional “first,” treatment advice, cure claim,
prevalence estimate, or automatic reform/progress story. Do not use a famous writer or named
case as a stand-in for poorer, racialized, Indigenous, migrant, enslaved, or otherwise absent
people. No public prose, images, SEO/GEO pages, scripts, reels, implementation, or publication.
The R18 Nahua/contact-zone lane remains `AUTHORITY_UNRESOLVED` and is not activated here.
