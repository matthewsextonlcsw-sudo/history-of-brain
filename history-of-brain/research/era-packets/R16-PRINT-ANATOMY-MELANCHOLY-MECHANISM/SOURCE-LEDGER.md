## Bounded design

Three settings are kept separate:

1. Vesalius, *Fabrica* Book VII, Padua–Basel print setting, 1543/1555.
2. Burton, *Anatomy of Melancholy*, 1621 witness with the 1621–1651 revision chain used only as edition context.
3. Descartes–Elisabeth, selected 1643/1645 letters, the 1649 *Passions*, and one controlled page from the posthumous 1664 *L’Homme* witness.
