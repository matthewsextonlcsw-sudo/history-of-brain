## Frozen corpus and claim ceiling

This packet is intentionally not a history of prehistoric “mental health.” It tests what three bounded archives permit:

1. Maloti–Drakensberg material evidence and the Qing explanations recorded and rearranged by Joseph Orpen in 1873–74.
2. Tsodilo's archaeological sequence, poorly dated paintings, and living Hambukushu and San sacred-landscape relationships as separate evidence layers.
3. The Digital Bleek–Lloyd ǀXam archive created from 1870 onward in colonial, carceral, domestic, philological, translation, and later digitization settings.

Twyfelfontein /Ui-//aes is excluded because no current Namibian community/custodial route was verified. Material observation, later interpretation, living authority, research permission, and reuse permission remain separate throughout.
