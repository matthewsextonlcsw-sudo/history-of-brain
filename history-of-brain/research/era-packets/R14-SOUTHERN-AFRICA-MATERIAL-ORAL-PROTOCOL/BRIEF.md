# R14-SOUTHERN-AFRICA-MATERIAL-ORAL-PROTOCOL — What the Archives Permit Us to Say

**State:** Authorized as protocol-and-source-feasibility research only

**Identifier range:** `027000–027999`

## Research question

What can selected southern African material and later-recorded oral archives responsibly
establish—and what must remain unknown—when dating, colonial mediation, living sacred-landscape
relationships, custody, and community authority are treated as evidence rather than footnotes?

## Frozen settings

1. Maloti–Drakensberg, South Africa/Lesotho: broadly dated rock paintings plus Qing's
   explanations recorded through Joseph Orpen in 1873–74 as a later colonial/translation seam.
2. Tsodilo Hills, Botswana: the long archaeological sequence, poorly dated paintings, and
   present Hambukushu and San sacred-landscape relationships kept separate.
3. Digital Bleek–Lloyd ǀXam archive, 1870s–1880s: named contributors, imprisonment/displacement,
   recording, translation, collectors, and dispersed custody; never precolonial testimony or a
   universal worldview.

Twyfelfontein /Ui-//aes remains outside the core unless a current Namibian community and
custodial permission route is verified.

## Claim ceiling and required tests

- document material features, dating limits, recording and collection histories, attributed
  interpretations, current custody, living relationships, and scholarly disputes;
- treat “trance,” “shamanism,” “healing,” altered consciousness, and continuity as attributed
  disputed interpretations, not direct readings, diagnoses, or efficacy claims;
- never use later Kalahari ethnography as an automatic decoder for older material;
- record which South African, Lesotho, Botswana, traditional/community, museum, heritage,
  management, and research authorities govern each proposed action;
- distinguish research access, image/scan reproduction, precise location, sacred knowledge,
  quotation, translation, public indexing, and commercial/GEO use; and
- audit universal “San psychology,” prehistoric therapy, direct patient experience,
  prevalence, efficacy, timeless continuity, and continent-wide inference.

## Hard holds

No ARADA rock-art images without written permission. Treat Bleek–Lloyd scans/images as
permission-required link-only material. Do not publish precise sensitive locations, off-trail
records, sacred knowledge, recordings, or community narratives without the relevant custodian
and community authority. The South African San Council route does not govern Botswana or
Namibia.
