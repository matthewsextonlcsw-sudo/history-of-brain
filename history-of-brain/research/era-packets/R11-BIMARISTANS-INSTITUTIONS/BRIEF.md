# R11-BIMARISTANS-INSTITUTIONS — Named Institutions, Endowments, Workers, and Recipients

**State:** Authorized; research only

**Identifier range:** `016000–016999`

## Research question

What can surviving evidence establish about the founding, space, funding, staffing, admission,
care, control, teaching, pharmacy, and recipients of a small set of named bimaristans or related
institutions between approximately 800 and 1400?

## Boundary

Choose no more than three named institutions in no more than two closely connected regions.
Every institution needs a separately dated evidence stack. Later descriptions, rebuilt sites,
modern plaques, and general hospital histories cannot stand in for contemporary documentation.

## Required evidence

- endowment documents, chronicles, administrative/legal records, archaeology, plans, objects,
  inscriptions, biographies, or other primary evidence with exact locators;
- current specialist institutional, urban, architectural, medical, labor, and legal history;
- separate evidence for building, endowment, staffing, teaching, pharmacy, ward organization,
  admission, mental-distress care, restraint, and outcome;
- who paid, governed, worked, entered, was excluded, left, died, resisted, or received support;
- recipient and worker voices, or an explicit account of their absence;
- distinctions among ideal, legal form, foundation story, later memory, and daily practice;
- audits of “first hospital,” “first psychiatric hospital,” universal humane care, music wards,
  occupational therapy, and chains that cite one another; and
- current site, custody, conflict, heritage-authority, and image/provenance cautions.

## Stop

Do not produce a global bimaristan list or infer psychiatric specialization from a room label or
late retelling. Hand household, shrine, and legal responses outside the named institutions to
the parallel packet. Create the five standard outputs and rights-safe ignored cache; no public
prose.
