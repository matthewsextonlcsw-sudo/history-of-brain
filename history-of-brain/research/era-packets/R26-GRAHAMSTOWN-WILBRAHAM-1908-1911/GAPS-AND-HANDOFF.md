## Replay protocol

For every acquired object, record repository, collection, series, volume/file, item, page/folio, image range, capture date, rights statement, and complete-parent status. Keep each original separate from the 2014 publication and from any repository metadata page.

### Patient letter and file

- Capture every page, side, cover, envelope, enclosure, blank-but-sequenced surface, and file-order marker.
- Record handwriting, pagination, deletions, additions, marginalia, stamps, annotations, cross-references, and differing hands without interpreting them prematurely.
- Produce an independent diplomatic transcription and compare it line by line with the published transcription.
- Determine whether “retained” and “not delivered” are supported by file order or annotations; preserve uncertainty about reason and policy.

### Casebook, administrative files, ledgers, estate, and register

- Capture title/cover and metadata pages plus the complete cited entry or document, including continuations and adjacent headings needed to interpret fields.
- For T/1207, establish the complete annexure set and sequence; do not use a detached page as the parent file.
- For HGM Vol. 28, define the name/case/date search and document negative as well as positive results. A ledger label does not prove a payment.
- For MOOC, distinguish inventory, account, correspondence, and administrative actions; do not turn estate administration into household experience.
- For the Cory register, capture the field definitions and entry context; an admission line is not a patient narrative.
- For G41–1909, open the complete parent report, contents, tables, notes, definitions, and relevant institutional sections before making any count or practice claim.
