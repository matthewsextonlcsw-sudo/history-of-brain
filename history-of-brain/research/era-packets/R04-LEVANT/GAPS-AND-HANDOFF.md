**Disposition:** Kirta is no longer part of the R04 researched core or outline-ready list. The
K. C. Hanson page provides only an incomplete dependent presentation of 1.14, and publisher
access to specialist Sha'tiqatu/Kirta studies was blocked. The conventional
fourteenth/thirteenth-century BCE placement also precedes this brief's approximately 1200 BCE
boundary. R04 does not expand its scope to absorb the gap.

**Do not yet claim:** the causal role of Kirta's vow, the exact relationship of royal illness to
land fertility, the identity/function of Sha'tiqatu, exact healing procedure, or outcome.

**Handoff requirement:** an earlier Bronze Age packet must obtain library access to a current
KTU/CAT edition, at least two complete scholarly translations, authoritative object/provenance
records, Lewis on Sha'tiqatu, and specialist counter-readings. Record tablet/column/line and
date basis for every claim. Until then, all Kirta propositions remain Unknown.
