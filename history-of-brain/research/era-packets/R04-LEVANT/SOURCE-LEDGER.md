## Retrieval, scope, and verification note

The named core corpus is deliberately limited to: (1) selected passages in the Hebrew Bible
across creation narrative, royal narrative, lament, wisdom, prophecy, and Aramaic apocalypse;
and (2) the Community Rule's Treatise on the Two Spirits, 1QS III.13–IV.26. Lachish Ostracon 3
is a small nonliterary control. The Ugaritic Kirta/Keret epic is now a **quarantined Bronze Age
prelude lead**, not part of the researched core: the available page is incomplete and the
epic's conventional fourteenth/thirteenth-century BCE setting falls before the brief's
approximately 1200 BCE boundary. Later rabbinic
and patristic corpora, a general history of Levantine demonology, retrospective diagnosis, and
claims about a single “biblical mind” are excluded.

Agent Reach was used through Jina Reader (`https://r.jina.ai/<URL>`) to open complete web
sources. Agent Reach's Exa route was unavailable, so discovery used the authorized built-in web
search fallback. Search snippets, abstracts, CAPTCHA pages, and publisher summaries are leads
only. Substantive claims below rely on opened full text, a critical edition, an authoritative
text database, or an object record. Access date for web resources is August 18, 2026.

Ratings are relationship-specific. `L1–L5/LX` identifies source level; `A–D` identifies fit to
the evidence job described. A text is L1-A for what it says, but not for the lived prevalence of
the idea it voices. The medieval Masoretic manuscript and modern translations are witnesses to
transmission, not automatic proof of the date or social reach of every passage.
