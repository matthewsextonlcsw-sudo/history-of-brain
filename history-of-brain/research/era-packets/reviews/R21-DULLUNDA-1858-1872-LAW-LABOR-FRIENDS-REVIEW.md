## Decision

The packet's central historical controls are strong. Independent replay supports its bounded
account of the 1858 statute as legal design rather than implementation; the 1867, 1869 and
1872 reports as one colonial administrative chain; institutional work and revenue as facts of
administrative accounting rather than proof of consent, coercion, therapy or benefit; and
friends and relatives as represented legal/economic actors rather than recovered family
voices. The packet also handles named release decisions, mortality, restraint, women's
accommodation, racialized/diagnostic terminology and image use with appropriate dignity and
harm safeguards.

The packet nevertheless must return before coordinator reconciliation. Five defects prevent a
reproducible evidence pass:

1. `SRC-033003` identifies the rejected Internet Archive object `b28149129` as a matching
   witness even though the ledger and cache correctly say that object is nonmatching;
2. the complete-1867 voice audit overlooks mediated affected-person speech in the same parent
   item and therefore states the archive-silence finding too broadly;
3. all six source `rights_status` fields use prose rather than a controlled value;
4. the frozen brief requires lawful full items to be cached and hashed, but the nine-page Act
   is link-only and has no reproducible local witness; and
5. the cache manifest proves aggregate integrity but lacks per-object provenance, rights and
   allowed-use fields, while one two-page evidence locator claims image replay although only
   one of its two page images is cached.

This is a bounded repair return, not a request to expand the packet into a general history of
colonial psychiatry or to produce public prose.

## Act XXXVI of 1858 replay

The official viewer currently resolves as a complete nine-page PDF. The packet's substantive
Act paraphrases pass:

- section III specifies at-least-monthly visitor inspection, examination of confined people,
  review of recent admission orders/certificates and an inspection entry;

  proceedings, and Presidency-town/Straits substitutions;

implementation. `CLM-033200–033202` and `CLM-033220` preserve that boundary.

family/friend or subordinate-worker quotation was identified in the inspected Dullunda
sections. Visitor decisions are official judgments; labor declarations and work descriptions

wording that no affected-person “statement” was identified anywhere in the complete item.

Repair `EVD-033123`, `CLM-033212`, `CLM-033214`, the voice matrix, archive-position prose and
handoff summary to distinguish three propositions: no direct first-person quotation was found;
no Dullunda affected-person/family/worker statement was found in the defined Dullunda
sections; and the complete 1867 parent item does contain out-of-scope, administratively
mediated speech traces. Do not publish the person's name by default. Keep the Cuttack material
out of substantive Dullunda claims unless the brief is formally changed.
