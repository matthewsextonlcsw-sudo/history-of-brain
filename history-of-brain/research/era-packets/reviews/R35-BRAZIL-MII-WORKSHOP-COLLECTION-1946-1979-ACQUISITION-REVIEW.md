It also separates present custody and heritage statements from historical possession, title, copyright, moral rights, privacy, creator consent and permission to narrate, exhibit or reproduce. These defects do not establish an alternative event date, ownership, consent, transfer, infringement or permission. They require source, evidence, claim and disagreement repairs before the dossier can receive a `PASS WITH GAPS` acquisition-only disposition. Nothing in this review opens public use.
