## Decision

The packet's central safety architecture passes. It defines the corpus before authority discovery,
keeps community authority separate from copyright, repository access, institutional ethics, and
legal compliance, prevents frameworks and directories from becoming permission, forbids project
self-assignment of Local Contexts TK/BC Labels, and leaves every substantive lane—including the
Nahua central-Mexico 1519–1606 proposal—at `AUTHORITY_UNRESOLVED` and **HOLD**.

The packet must nevertheless return for three source-exact repairs:

1. The National Inuit Strategy on Research is represented too weakly as national coordination.
   The strategy says consent should be sought through Inuit representational organizations or
   Inuit-appointed institutions and calls Inuit representational organizations the rightful
   gatekeepers of Inuit Nunangat research. A national document does not itself authorize this
   project, but the relevant national, regional, sub-regional, or appointed Inuit body may be an
   actual authority when its mandate covers the defined corpus and use.
2. Several NAGPRA summaries blur the regulation's applicability actors with the narrower actors
   bound by the § 10.1(d) duty of care. Section 10.1(b) includes Indian Tribes on Tribal lands in
   its applicability provision; §§ 10.1(c)–(d) bind museums, Federal agencies, and DHHL to the
   systematic-process and duty-of-care obligations. The consent-before-access/research rule is
   otherwise correctly stated and bounded to covered human remains or cultural items.
3. The Local Contexts record omits the current Hub/account gate. The FAQ, last updated
   2026-07-24, says Notices can only be generated and applied through subscribed institution or
   researcher accounts, while Labels can only be applied through a confirmed community account
   by authorized community representatives. Disclosure/Engagement Notices may not require
   community approval, but they still require the authorized Hub workflow and remain disclosure,
   never permission.

These are bounded governance-precision repairs. They do not authorize a cultural packet, outreach,
source opening, extraction, translation, publication, or Local Contexts implementation.

## Mandatory repair group 1 — Inuit authority and NISR

### Source-exact problem

`SRC-029003`, `EVD-029004`, and the authority map retain the correct warning that a national
strategy is not automatic approval, but they omit the strategy's pivotal authority language. In
particular:

- NISR printed p. 12 says researchers and institutions must seek Inuit FPIC by engaging Inuit
  representational organizations or Inuit-appointed institutions;
- printed pp. 13–16 name regional organizations, research licensing bodies, and approval routes;
- printed p. 29 says Inuit representational organizations should be engaged as partners in
  governance; and
- printed p. 33 calls Inuit representational organizations the rightful gatekeepers of Inuit
  Nunangat research and says consent must precede use/publication of Inuit-specific data.

The existing statement that “ITK or NISR alone” cannot authorize is too categorical when ITK or
another representational organization may be the relevant mandated body for a particular national,
regional, or cross-regional corpus. The safe rule is not “national cannot authorize”; it is “the
document alone does not authorize, and the relevant body's actual mandate must cover this corpus and
each proposed use.”

### Exact repair

1. Expand `EVD-029004` or add a new in-range evidence record with locators at NISR pp. 12–16,
   29, and 33. Preserve the separate 2018–2022 implementation horizon.
2. Revise `SRC-029003` scope/limitations and `CLM-029103`'s supporting note so that Inuit
   representational organizations and appointed institutions are possible actual authorities within
   their verified mandates; NISR remains a framework/source, not the authorization record itself.
3. Revise the Inuit lane and NISR framework row in `AUTHORITY-AND-JURISDICTION-MAP.md`:
   identify the relevant national, regional, sub-regional, licensing, community, family, or other
   body for the exact corpus and do not automatically require a “local” body where a demonstrated
   representational mandate properly governs the use.
4. Keep the future Inuit lane at `AUTHORITY_UNRESOLVED` and **HOLD**. No corpus, affected-party
   map, delegated mandate, or use-specific decision currently exists.
5. Rerun relationship coverage/dependence if a new EVD/REL ID is allocated. If `EVD-029004` is
   amended atomically, retain `REL-029205` but update its explanation and claim-fit wording.
