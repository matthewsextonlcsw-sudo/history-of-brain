## Decision

The packet has a strong, appropriately cautious historical core. Independent replay confirms the
composite structure of Packard's 1868 publication, Packard's editorial intervention before
Olsen's separately attributed narrative, the shared publication chain of the four named testimony
sections, the exact 1867 statute, the committee's procedure and credibility gate, its selective
publication/privacy reasoning, and the Board of Public Charities' later liberty/admission tension.
The packet also correctly refuses sole-causation, implementation, prevalence, efficacy,
firstness, modern-diagnosis, unmediated-family-voice, and universal-experience claims.

It nevertheless cannot enter reconciliation yet. Seven evidence records use noncanonical
directness values, six use claim-sensitivity labels in the evidence-sensitivity field, and three
claims use noncanonical universality casing. The frozen complete committee-report gate is not
met: the ledger says only printed pp. 3–79 were replayed although the hospital report continues
through printed p. 81. Two modern scholarly routes do not meet the project's full-text and exact-
locator rule as recorded. One current-science claim lacks the evidence burden for that claim type,
and the family archival-silence claim lacks an explicit corpus-wide search record. Finally, all
cached payload hashes pass, but the cache lacks the per-object source/provenance/rights manifest
required to reproduce its OCR, metadata, and selected-page derivatives.

These are repairable defects. They do not overturn the packet's substantive holds or authorize
public writing.

## Pivotal source replay

The complete cached object is a 504-page scan. Direct image/OCR replay confirms:

- preface pp. v–vi describe the appended contributors and use the connected Illinois committee's

  plan, not independently verified circulation or influence.

`EVD-040000–007` and `CLM-040000–004`, `CLM-040009` therefore survive substantive replay. The
packet's author/editor/contributor distinctions and dependence notes are essential and must remain.

### 1867 Personal Liberty Act

Printed pp. 139–140 visibly contain the act title and sections 1–6, including the jury-verdict and

does not establish notice, access, enforcement, experience, protection rate, or Packard's causal
authorship; `CLM-040008` and the existing implementation hold remain correct.

### Investigating committee report

Direct replay confirms the committee's mandate and private procedure at printed pp. 3–6, the
complaint/defense structure, the Packard section, the historical witness-competency discussion at
pp. 55–58, and the conclusion/privacy discussion at pp. 77–79. The committee says it examined

powerful but selective state evidence apparatus, not a neutral transcript or complete adjudication.

The source-full gate is incomplete, however. The hospital report continues through printed
pp. 80–81 with the committee's position on institutional purpose, claimed population estimates,

source-limit analysis before the frozen “complete official report” requirement is satisfied. The

`EVD-040011` also uses “approximately pp. 48–55.” The selected image shows the “Mrs. Packard's
Case” heading on printed p. 47. Replace the approximate locator with the exact verified boundary
after full section collation; do not retain an approximate range in a source-verified record.
