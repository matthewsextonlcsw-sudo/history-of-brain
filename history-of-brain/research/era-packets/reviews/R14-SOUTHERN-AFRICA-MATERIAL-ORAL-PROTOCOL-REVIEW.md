## Decision

The packet gets the difficult historical boundaries substantially right. It keeps rock paint,
stratigraphy, motifs, institutional description, oral attribution, and scholarly interpretation in
different evidentiary layers. It makes the military and employment setting of Orpen's exchange with
Qing visible, does not present the published English as verbatim speech, and does not use Bleek's
later comparison as neutral community adjudication. It also keeps Tsodilo's long but noncontinuous
material record separate from the broad and uncertain painting chronology and from present
Hambukushu and San relationships. The trance/healing dispute is attributed rather than resolved by
fiat, and no side is turned into a prehistoric clinical, efficacy, or patient-experience claim.

The packet must nevertheless return before internal outline extraction. The replay found exact
source metadata errors, wrong PDF locators for the pivotal Orpen disclosures, a false “named elder”
statement, a changing-webpage/source mismatch, uncontrolled evidence values, one claim/graph
array mismatch, and a relationship table that cannot reproduce Batch 05's claim-relative source
level, A–D fit, or dependence test. `CLM-027206` also calls a 2013–2015 published exchange “live”
without a current distribution review. `CLM-027224` is a project clearance decision but is typed and
linked as if its supporting sources could prove the project's permission state. These are internal,
feasible repairs, not reasons to broaden the regional scope or relax any hold.

The packet's current `PASS WITH GAPS` headers are therefore premature under the rule that packet
authors cannot close their own work. They may be restored only after the repairs below and an
independent closure replay. The present-day community, custodian, rights, language, legal, and
specialist gaps are separate publication gates; desk repair cannot manufacture those authorities.

## Material observation, interpretation, and lived authority

The packet passes the central material-versus-interpretation test after the identified record repairs:

- physical paint, motif observation, stratigraphy, material association, radiocarbon constraints,
  and heritage corpus estimates are not treated as direct belief, emotion, diagnosis, therapy, or
  outcome;
- Tsodilo's occupation duration is not used to date paintings or prove a continuous community;
- the horse/rider observation, inferred terminus, and reported N/aekhwe attribution are recognized
  as different layers, although `EVD-027115` must be made structurally atomic;
- UNESCO's spiritual/sacred language is attributed to its state-party heritage synthesis rather
  than presented as self-evident from an image;
- current community relationships are meaningful in the present without being used as automatic
  proof of particular-image authorship; and
- neither the pro-trance nor critical scholarly position is translated into modern psychiatric
  diagnosis, treatment efficacy, prevalence, or a recipient's experience.

The colonial-mediation audit also passes in substance. Qing is named, while Orpen's military role,
guide recruitment, unnamed translators, rearrangement, and uncertainty travel with the record.
Bleek–Lloyd contributors retain names and intellectual agency while prison, release, household,
age, translation, photography/measurement, dependency, collector selection, and dispersed custody
remain visible. The archive does not tell us how a prehistoric person felt, whether anyone sought
care, whether an action was welcome or coercive, or whether it helped or harmed them. The packet
correctly refuses to invent satisfaction, fear, trauma, cure, or wellness from silence.

Living authority remains a hard external gap. UNESCO, a state permit, an expired management target,
a university archive, a copyright license, ARADA permission, or the South African San Council's
South African remit cannot substitute for the relevant present Hambukushu, San, Lesotho, site,
custodial, asset, language, and individual authorities for a particular action.

dependency note. It therefore does not record the required claim-relative rating or permit a
reproducible false-corroboration audit. Rebuild all 64 rows with explicit fields for:

- relationship ID;
- evidence ID;
- controlled predicate;
- claim ID;
- edge-specific `source_level` (`L1–L5/LX`);

This is not satisfied by a source's general `claim_fit` prose or by evidence-level strings that list
multiple fits. The same evidence can be A-fit for reporting what an institution said and D-fit for
