## Decision

The packet has a strong, responsibly bounded research core. Full-source replay supports the
three selected carrier/witness problems, the major object and passage locators, the distinction
between source terms and modern diagnostic English, the normative-versus-implemented-care
limit, the dependency groups, and the cache manifest. No evidence was found that warrants
discarding any of the three chains.

It must nevertheless return before coordinator reconciliation. The principal blockers are:

1. the source, evidence, and claim tables do not implement the required controlled fields;
2. several records combine independent propositions or multiple source layers in one ID;
3. the *Bhaiṣajyavastu* care passage is worded more broadly than the actually opened
   Tibetan-based English witness;
4. selected textual variants are used too close to physical-route evidence;
5. several relationship verbs and evidence labels overstate what modern reception sources or
   custody facts prove; and
6. the modern myth audits are useful bounded safeguards but not yet complete origin/continuity
   chains.

This is a bounded repair return, not a request to expand R12 into a history of Buddhism or Asia.

## Record-architecture audit

### Source records

The compact ledger and three-range crosswalk do not satisfy the canonical source schema.
`source_type` currently contains uncontrolled subtype phrases such as
`manuscript_catalog`, `critical_digital_edition`, and
`specialist_textual_and_codicological_study`. Normalize each row to a controlled type
(`primary_textual`, `primary_material`, `scholarly_synthesis`,
`institutional_reference`, `community_knowledge`, `discovery_lead`, etc.) and put the existing
specific phrase in `subtype`.

Every `SRC` record must explicitly resolve creator role, creation/publication date distinction,
edition/version, container, publisher/holder, consulted/original language, translator,

population, provenance, rights, cultural protocol, source position, limitations, independence,
opened by/on, and controlled verification status. `SRC-018010` must remain visibly `lead` or
`rejected`, with the access failure recorded.

### Evidence records

The compact evidence table omits required evidence form, translation/translator, original-text
handling, context, speaker/subject, recorder/editor, method, directness, representativeness,
limitations, sensitivity, researcher note, extractor/date, and controlled verification status.
These are material in this packet because nearly every pivotal claim turns on mediation,
translation, represented voice, or absence.

Do not use a single evidence ID for separate propositions or distinct source jobs:

- split `EVD-018107` into Wujastyk's date proposal and the Bodleian custody record;
- split `EVD-018109` into the Sanskrit transcription and Hoernle's dated English layer, then
  connect them without claiming independence;

  rather than pretending “all three chains” is a single source.

`EVD-018110` also needs an explicit bounded corpus/method record so selected-source absence is
not mistaken for historical absence.

### Claim records

The claim table omits controlled `claim_type`, time, geography, population, universality,
controlled evidence label, current-evidence note where relevant, anachronism risk, sensitivity,
created by/on, and controlled review status. Mixed status phrases such as “Strongly supported
as packet method” are not valid substitutes for `evidence_label` plus `review_status`.

claims with different sources, fit, uncertainty, and review burdens. Preserve the old ID for one
proposition and allocate a new in-range claim ID for the other; update every dependent
