# Independent cross-review — R03-EGYPT

**Packet reviewed:** `R03-EGYPT — Heart, Brain, Body, Family, and Archive`

**Reviewer role:** independent packet cross-review
**Disposition:** **PASS WITH GAPS**
**Publication status:** no public prose or public claim promotion authorized

## Decision

R03's central source conclusions survive risk-based replay. Edwin Smith Case 6 documents a
severe head wound and exposed internal tissue in Breasted's edition but not a civilization-wide
brain theory. Papyrus Ebers 854–855 supports passage-specific `jb`/`ḥꜣ.tj`, vessel, palpation,
affect, and practitioner claims while resisting one-to-one modern translation. Herodotus 2.86 and
the 2024 child-mummy CT study support variation in excerebration practice and do not provide a
belief or motive for tissue removal. The Hu, *Dialogue*, and Heqanakht materials are appropriately
distinguished as a ritual first-person appeal, a literary speaker, and a husband's household
commands rather than merged into “patient experience.”

The packet does not require wholesale return for new research. It does require source-record,
locator, evidence-label, and wording repairs before coordinator acceptance as a verified packet.
The most consequential are a wrong DOI, three source records that aggregate or duplicate works,
unstable PLOS locators, an unresolved kinship/translator issue in UC 16244, and the absence of
typed evidence–claim relationship records. The packet's already-declared modern Edwin Smith,
Egyptian-language, late-antique, and human-remains gaps remain publication blockers.
