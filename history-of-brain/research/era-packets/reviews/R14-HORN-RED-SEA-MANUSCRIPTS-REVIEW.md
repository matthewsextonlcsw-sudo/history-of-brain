## Decision

The packet's historical ceiling is responsible and should be preserved. It does not turn four
manuscript settings into a Horn-wide system, manuscript survival into implementation, a sacred
biography into autobiography or a clinical case, “spiritual medicine” into psychotherapy, or a
former Harar shelfmark into copy place, identity, or practice. Its strongest source-layer
distinctions survived independent replay.

The packet must nevertheless return before internal-outline extraction. The IES00272 database has
two unrecorded internal conflicts at the exact folio/date on which `CLM-025215` rests; its named
cataloger is also missing from the canonical source record. Eight source-level fields are not
controlled values, the relationship rating column mixes the two rating axes, `REL-025334` reverses
the meaning of its claim, and three disagreement records do not currently represent credible
competing interpretations. Two “Strongly supported” claims count sources that support different
halves of compound claims rather than independent corroboration of one proposition. The named-owner
prayer's translator is inferred rather than documented. These are bounded reproducibility and graph
repairs, not reasons to expand the packet.
