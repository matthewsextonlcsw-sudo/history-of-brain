## Mandatory repair 1 — represent the omitted BMJ worker, landlord, and organizer-result lanes

The packet says the complete BMJ article was replayed, but its graph stops using the article at
pp.1389–1390. BMJ pp.1392–1394 contain material directly relevant to R33's frozen labor, rent,
worker, outcome, consent, and institutional-power questions:

1. On p.1393 Lambo reports that much of the hospital labor force came from surrounding villages
   and that the laborers were landlords of most patients living in those villages. He then
   describes their relationship to hospital group activities. The grammar and scope require
   careful extraction; this does not establish wages, rent terms, voluntariness, patient labor,
   fair exchange, or any worker's own view.
2. On pp.1392–1394 Lambo advances positive claims about day-hospital benefit, advantages over
   hospitalization, recovery/readaptation, village tolerance, and justification for larger-scale
   adoption. These are organizer assertions from his own program, not independently measured
   outcomes. They belong in the same organizer dependence group and need explicit sample,
   comparator, definition, follow-up, adverse-event, and dissent limitations.
3. On p.1394 Lambo thanks government authorities for permission to use hospital clinical
   material. Institutional permission is source-provenance evidence; it is not evidence of
   patient consent, privacy clearance, or present-day publication permission.

Add atomic EVD/CLM/REL records for these source positions. Keep the worker/landlord statement
separate from patient occupational activity. Do not infer that landlords represented all hosts,
that employment made the arrangement consensual, that hospital group participation was paid or
voluntary, or that the article's positive conclusions establish efficacy. Update the lived-
experience and handoff files so the new evidence is visible but every existing labor, rent,
consent, family, outcome, privacy, and public-use hold remains intact.

## Mandatory repair 4 — make the UN outcome paraphrase source-exact

`EVD-045010` and `CLM-045014` say the UN reported that the scheme “had proved beneficial” and
“reduced hospital-induced symptoms.” The pp.87–88 passage does not use the first formulation and
does not report a measured reduction. It calls the system new and low-cost, presents minimized
dislocation/no readjustment as an asset, and says the scheme “has proved” that community-treated
patients often fail to develop certain symptoms described as apparently induced by hospital
conditions.

Reword the evidence and claim to those exact attributed positions. Retain the present method
gaps and the ban on efficacy, recovery, relapse, superiority, preference, stigma, and causal
language. F/121 remains unopened and must not be backfilled from the UN synthesis.
