## Decision

The packet's historical boundaries are strong. It keeps Unguja material observation separate from
object function, refuses to turn an inaccessible amulet/seal study into a wearer or treatment story,
dates the Mombasa evidence layers, credits Thomas Govi as a later intellectual rather than a
sixteenth-century eyewitness, treats UNESCO as a protocol cue rather than community permission,
and keeps the *Utendi wa Tambuka* attribution/date/witness/edition chain visibly mediated. It also
states the most important lived-experience result correctly: this opened corpus contains no direct
pre-1800 affected-person account of distress, care, coercion, treatment, or outcome.

The packet must nevertheless return before internal outline extraction. Five claims use
`Strongly supported` even though the graph contains only one source or dependent repetitions for
their exact propositions. Most importantly, Bresnahan's semantic list for *uganga* explicitly cites
Ray p. 117, so `EVD-026107` and `EVD-026110` are not independent confirmations. The typed
relationship table does not carry exact per-edge `source_level`, one A–D `claim_fit`, normalized
dependence group, or active/withdrawn status even though the project says ratings are
source-claim-relative. The 1728 disagreement record omits `EVD-026121`, includes the separate
author/commission evidence instead, lacks required reviewer/date fields, and is also linked from an
authorship/commission claim that its date-only question does not adjudicate. These are graph and
claim-burden defects, not reasons to broaden the packet.

The current `PASS WITH GAPS` headers are therefore premature under Batch 05's rule that packet
authors cannot approve their own work. They may be restored only after the repairs and an
independent closure replay. All existing public, quotation, object, language, custody, and community
holds remain in force.
