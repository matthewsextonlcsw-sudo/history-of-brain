## Safe handoff propositions

- A specific medieval household letter can document what one relative asked another to do; it
  cannot establish that the request was followed.
- A legal rule can partition consequences by a historical capacity category without describing a
  disease or proving how courts acted.
- A shrine deed can document a formal exchange of narrated healing, provision, service, and
  obedience; it cannot by itself recover the child's experience.
- The packet's heterogeneity is sufficient only as a myth-risk safeguard: these universal stories
  are not supportable from this exact corpus. The packet has not completed their popular reception
  chains, cannot calculate prevalence, and cannot propose a new universal.
