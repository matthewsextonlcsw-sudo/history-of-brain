# R11-HOUSEHOLDS-LAW-SHRINES — Family, Law, Shrines, Community Care, and Confinement

**State:** Authorized; research only

**Identifier range:** `017000–017999`

## Research question

Outside named hospitals, what can a bounded set of legal, household, shrine, charitable,
biographical, and material sources from approximately 750 to 1400 establish about how families,
judges, healers, religious communities, neighbors, and rulers responded to troubling conduct,
distress, disability, incapacity, possession, wandering, poverty, and care?

## Boundary

Select no more than three named legal or social settings and a small primary corpus. The packet
may use Arabic, Persian, Syriac, Hebrew, Coptic, or other relevant evidence only with named
regional and language boundaries. Law, hagiography, medicine, shrine practice, and household
records remain different archives.

## Required evidence

- opened legal responsa/manuals/court records, household or biographical material, shrine or
  charitable evidence, and material records where available;
- specialist social, legal, disability, religious, gender, family, and medical history;
- exact historical vocabulary with translation disputes and no modern diagnostic substitution;
- distinction between legal capacity, moral/religious category, social conflict, and medical
  description;
- implementation evidence separate from normative rule;
- family labor, gender, age, status, enslavement/dependence, poverty, cost, consent, refusal,
  violence, confinement, and community support;
- explicit audit of who records the event and whether the affected person's words survive; and
- reception-chain audits of universal family care, universal tolerance, demonology-only, and
  religion-versus-medicine stories.

## Stop

Do not treat one legal school, community, shrine, or household story as representative of the
Islamicate world. Do not import hospital facts. Create the five standard outputs and rights-safe
ignored cache; no public prose.
