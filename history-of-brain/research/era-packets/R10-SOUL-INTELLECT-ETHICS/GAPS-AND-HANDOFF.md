## Executive disposition

The packet supports an internal outline built around **four named Arabic-writing authors and their disagreements**, not a history of “Islamic psychology.” Its strongest anchors are:

- al-Kindī's fourfold intellect list and the fragility of its manuscript transmission;
- al-Fārābī's directly opened heart/body/city hierarchy, specialist-mediated brain and faculty claims, imagination, and philosophical account of revelation;
- Ibn Sīnā's internal senses, brain-locus model, floating-person thought experiment, intellect stages, and differentiated prophecy arguments; and
- al-Ghazālī's ethical habituation, governance of anger/desire, and selective use/critique of philosophy.

No patient/practice history, modern diagnostic continuity, or priority claim is ready. No public prose was written.

6. **Move from knowing to becoming:** al-Ghazālī's habituation, anger/desire, and selective philosophical engagement (`CLM-014014`–`CLM-014016`).
