# R10-SOUL-INTELLECT-ETHICS findings and evidence records

Status: internal research synthesis; not public prose.
Research date: 2026-08-18.

## Comparative finding

The useful through-line is not “the Islamic discovery of psychology.” It is a sequence of disagreements and repurposings among four Arabic-writing authors:

1. **al-Kindī:** a compact intellect taxonomy and a soul discourse assembled in a fragile surviving corpus, with Greek doxographic material whose exact sources are not always recoverable.
2. **al-Fārābī:** a hierarchy of bodily and cognitive faculties in which imagination links sensation, desire, sleep, representation, politics, and revelation; the opened translated excerpt directly establishes the heart/body/city hierarchy, while the brain's secondary role and some faculty details remain specialist-mediated through López-Farjeat's Walzer passage map.
3. **Ibn Sīnā:** a more subdivided account of internal senses, bodily organs, intellectual acquisition, self-awareness, and prophetic capacity; the “floating person” is an argument, not a laboratory experiment.
4. **al-Ghazālī:** ethical formation through disciplined repetition, reason, desire, anger, and religious ends; this is neither a simple rejection of philosophy nor evidence of clinical psychotherapy.

These texts allow history of arguments. They do not show what most people believed, how patients experienced distress, or whether a technique worked.

## Disagreement records

### `DIS-014000` — sources and meaning of al-Kindī's soul/intellect accounts

- **Question:** How directly do al-Kindī's classifications reproduce Aristotle or other Greek sources?
- **Positions/evidence:** `EVD-014000` shows an attributed fourfold list; `EVD-014002` records Adamson's caution that some doxographic quotations/sources are opaque.
- **Reason / disposition:** Arabic transmission, paraphrase, lost Greek materials, and philosophical adaptation complicate source identification. Use “al-Kindī presents” and name the edition; do not say “Aristotle taught” or “al-Kindī invented” without passage-specific proof. Consensus degree not established in this packet.

### `DIS-014001` — al-Fārābī on immortality/conjunction

- **Question:** Did al-Fārābī deny individual immortality or conjunction with the Active Intellect?
- **Positions/evidence:** Later reports about a lost *Nicomachean Ethics* commentary suggest a denial; surviving works appear to pull differently (`EVD-014007`).
- **Reason / disposition:** The pivotal text is lost and all positions rely on later reporting plus reconstruction. Mark disputed; do not declare a final doctrine.

### `DIS-014002` — Ibn Sīnā's “proof” of prophecy

- **Question:** Does Ibn Sīnā's psychology prove prophecy or explain its possibility?
- **Positions/evidence:** `EVD-014012` describes exceptional faculties; `EVD-014013` distinguishes possibility/explanation from the separate necessity argument.
- **Reason / disposition:** Different works answer different argumentative questions. Write “offers a philosophical account of possibility and, in a separate treatise, an attempted necessity argument,” subject to specialist review.

### `DIS-014003` — al-Ghazālī and philosophy

- **Question:** Is al-Ghazālī's ethical psychology a rejection of philosophy?
- **Positions/evidence:** `EVD-014014`–`EVD-014016` show appropriation of virtue/habituation vocabulary within theological and revelatory ends, alongside criticism of particular doctrines.
- **Reason / disposition:** Corpus and genre matter. Avoid both “destroyed reason” and “simply continued Greek philosophy.”

### `DIS-014004` — psychologist/cognitive-scientist/psychotherapy priority labels

- **Question:** Can one of these authors be called the first psychologist, first cognitive scientist, or inventor/father of psychotherapy?
- **Positions/evidence:** `EVD-014018` documents a much later institutional psychology boundary; `EVD-014019` is an inaccessible popular lead; `EVD-014020` shows a rival modern honorific attached to al-Rāzī without criteria; `EVD-014024` is the packet's actual bounded priority search audit; `EVD-014010` is a thought experiment rather than an experiment.
- **Reason / disposition:** The labels lack stable criteria, collapse disciplinary chronology, and are not established by ancient passages. All priority claims are quarantined.
