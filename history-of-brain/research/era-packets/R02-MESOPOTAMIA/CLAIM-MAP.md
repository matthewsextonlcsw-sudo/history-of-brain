## Mandatory citation bundles for outline writers

- **Terms bundle:** SRC-002000 exact page + exact ancient text/line where available; add
  SRC-002004 when bodily/social context matters.
- **Healing bundle:** SRC-002003 or SRC-002004 exact page and text identifier; do not cite a
  general history page for a recipe or ritual. State that the text prescribes or represents
  the action; material use/frequency remains GAP-002012.
- ***Ludlul* bundle:** SRC-002001 exact chapter/line + SRC-002002 for genre. Never use the
  literary translation without the genre citation.
- **Archive bundle:** SRC-002007 exact page + the particular tablet's provenance/date from
  its edition.
- **“First” bundle:** the proposing source + the contradictory methodological source. The
  unqualified priority statement remains rejected even when both are cited.
- **Secondary-edition bundle:** BAM 202, BAM 316, BAM 323, KAR 32, Suālu IV, CT 4 8a,
  and LKA 154/157 remain attributed outline mappings through SRC-002000/003/004. Do not use
  their wording as L1 public quotation until the named critical edition, translator/editor,
  witness, and relevant variants are opened.

## Claims not authorized from this packet

- prevalence or incidence of any condition;
- a Mesopotamian DSM/ICD diagnosis;
- a universal belief shared across 2,700 years and all cities/languages;
- proof that a treatment worked or did not work;
- hospitals, wards, confinement systems, or professional psychotherapy;
- transparent patient testimony from *Ludlul* or a ritual “I”;
- a direct line from Mesopotamia to Greek, Islamic, or modern psychiatry; or
- “the first psychiatry,” “the first psychiatrist,” or “the discovery of mental illness.”
