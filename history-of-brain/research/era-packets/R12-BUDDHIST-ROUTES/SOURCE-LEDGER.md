## Method and source boundary

The packet retains three chains only:

1. CKM 77, a Gāndhārī/Kharoṣṭhī *Dharmapada* scroll **reported** from near Khotan;
2. the multi-text, multi-hand Bower bundle purchased at Kucha in 1890, especially the Sanskrit
   *Navanītaka*; and
3. the Gilgit/additional Sanskrit, Yijing Chinese, and Tibetan witness problem for the
   Mūlasarvāstivāda *Bhaiṣajyavastu*, plus one modern Tibetan-based English care passage.

These are not one route. Find report, purchase, manuscript date, copying place, current custody,
textual agreement, translation, reading, and use remain separate propositions.

Agent Reach v1.5.0 reported Jina Reader available. Exa was unavailable because its `mcporter`
route was not configured. Jina/full-document, direct institutional, and primary/scholarly search
fallbacks were used. Snippets and abstracts were leads only. `opened_by` is “R12 Codex packet
researcher” for all rows; `opened_on` and `accessed_on` are 2026-08-18 unless stated otherwise.
