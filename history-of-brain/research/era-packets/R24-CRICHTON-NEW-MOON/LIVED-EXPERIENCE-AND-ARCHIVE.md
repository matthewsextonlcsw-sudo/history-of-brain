## Editorial and production power

*New Moon* is valuable because affected-person-positioned writing survives within the institution.
That does not make the publication transparent. The archive object and issues preserve a product
shaped by editors, selection, pseudonymity, printing capacity, medical authority, institutional
reputation, class separation and survival. Contribution and control are different claims.

The Commission provides a second source position: it reports patient contributions while also
judging amusement, labor, staffing and accommodation. Its report should challenge a celebratory
reading of the journal, but it is not an affected-person countervoice. It is another authority
record.

## Class and access

The 1849 separation is not background decoration. The Commission's appendix records separate
private and pauper institutions and a private fee schedule tied to different rooms, food,
attendance and privileges (`CLM-036012`, `CLM-036013`). Any future contribution census must code,
where evidence allows, whether a writer/readership/printing arrangement belongs to Crichton,
Southern Counties, both, or remains unknown. It must not assume one equal “patient community.”

## Labor and work

- Gardening by twelve private gentlemen is an official report, not proof of voluntary or
  therapeutic work (`CLM-036009`).
- Commissioner preference for productive labor is a historical position, not a project judgment
  (`CLM-036010`).
- Staff numbers, wages, training and performance roles describe institutional organization, not
  workers' own experience (`CLM-036014`, `CLM-036020`).
- Adam Richardson's skilled-printer/employment lane remains entirely held because the exact
  correspondence is absent. No professionalization narrative may be inferred from the 1857
  editorial transition.

## Privacy, dignity and harm controls

- No patient names, addresses, family allegations, clinical case images, diagnoses, restraint,
  self-harm, intimate detail or re-identifying references were extracted.
- The single affected-person-positioned item remains `sensitive`; a historical public issue does
  not waive dignity, contextualization or commercial-use review.
- Historical labels such as “lunatic,” “pauper,” and institutional “class” terms belong to named
  sources. They are not current diagnoses or neutral identities.
- Do not use distressing facsimiles as atmosphere, spectacle, a thumbnail, a reel hook or an SEO
  device.

## Rights and repository controls

- `DGH1` *New Moon* images are marked CC BY-NC 4.0, and the catalog directs commercial
  reproduction questions to Dumfries and Galloway Archives and Local Studies. Couch Commons is
  link-only by default until written commercial-use and reproduction decisions are obtained.
- The Commission scan is Public Domain Mark 1.0, but legal availability does not remove patient,
  worker, dignity or contextual-harm review.
- Statutory text can be cited and linked; law remains an authority layer rather than visual proof
  of lived experience.
