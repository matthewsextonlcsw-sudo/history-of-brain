## Bounded findings

1. The inaugural December 1844 issue presents a self-conscious institutional editorial program.
   It is direct evidence for the program printed in the issue, not proof of free expression,
   patient control, or later editorial practice (`EVD-036003`, `CLM-036003`).
2. The frozen 1846-48 stratum is predeclared as the complete April 3, 1846 issue at b22667611
   canvases 65-68, but it is quarantined because its all-contribution census and item-level coding
   remain incomplete. Surviving mastheads also verify publication in July 1849 and January 1856.
   The 1849 issue is temporally adjacent to the institutional separation described by the
   Commission, but no causal or implementation inference is permitted (`EVD-036004`,
   `EVD-036005`, `EVD-036020`, `CLM-036004`, `CLM-036018`).
3. A January 1856 item uses first-person collective positioning as “fellow patients” while asking
   for pictures for galleries. It is an affected-person-positioned contribution inside an edited
   institution-supported publication; author identity, selection, response, and representativeness
   remain unknown (`EVD-036006`, `CLM-036006`).
4. October and November 1857 issues continued after the Act's August enactment. The closing
   editorial speaks of the periodical's phases and a transition, but the images do not establish
   the successor editorial/printer arrangement or show that law caused the transition
   (`EVD-036007`, `EVD-036008`, `CLM-036007`, `CLM-036008`).
5. The Commission reported a sharp private/pauper institutional separation: the 1849 Southern
   Counties Asylum received pauper patients and the original Crichton building thereafter received
   private patients. It also described rate-linked rooms, food, attendance and privileges
   (`EVD-036012`-`EVD-036014`, `CLM-036012`, `CLM-036013`).
6. The Commission described gardening by twelve private gentlemen, institutional amusements,
   printed periodicals with some patient contributions, and mixed-house recreation. These are
   official observations and judgments, not evidence of voluntariness, therapeutic effect, or
   equal access (`EVD-036009`-`EVD-036011`, `EVD-036016`, `CLM-036009`-`CLM-036011`).
7. The Act created a central lunacy board and inspection powers and specifically obligated the
   Crichton trustees/directors to receive qualifying pauper patients under stated conditions.
   Statutory design is not implementation evidence (`EVD-036017`-`EVD-036019`,
   `CLM-036015`-`CLM-036017`).
8. The existing cache cannot satisfy the frozen corpus's 24-30-item six-stratum contribution
   census: the April 1846 issue boundary is exact but its contribution census is incomplete, and
   the 1862-63 issue/item locator remains unresolved. Annual-report strata, twelve coded case
   records, the staff register, and Richardson correspondence are also absent. Missing evidence
   stays `Unknown`; it is not filled from adjacent institutional print (`EVD-036020`,
   `CLM-036018`-`CLM-036021`).
