## Route and cache audit

- This takeover used only the already present ignored cache. No browsing, downloading, outreach,
  or access-control workaround occurred.
- The cache contains complete 1,354-canvas and 630-canvas IIIF image runs for two `DGH1/7/1/1`
  *New Moon* objects, their manifests, a complete 945-page Royal Commission scan plus OCR, a
  complete 54-page enacted-statute PDF, and ten selected Commission page images.
- OCR was a finding aid. Every active *New Moon* and Commission evidence locator below was replayed
  against its named image. The statute was checked against the complete enacted PDF.
- No annual report, case record, staff engagement register, or Richardson correspondence is
  present in the cache. No such record is promoted from the brief or from memory.
- The 1862-63 image run is complete at object level, but an exact issue/item locator was not
  reproducibly established during the bounded takeover. All 1862-63 content claims remain `HOLD`.
- The frozen 1846-48 stratum is now predeclared as the complete April 3, 1846 issue in
  `DGH1/7/1/1/1`, b22667611 canvases 65-68; canvas 65 carries the dated masthead and canvas 69
  begins the next dated issue. The issue boundary is identified, but its all-contribution census,
  contribution boundaries and item-level writer/genre/editor/print-labor/uncertainty fields were
  not completed. The stratum is therefore quarantined from sampling and substantive use.
