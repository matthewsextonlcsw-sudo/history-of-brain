# R24 Crichton and the New Moon — Research Brief

**Status:** Frozen candidate for Batch 06 internal research; no public prose

**Place and dates:** Crichton Royal Institution and Southern Counties Asylum, Dumfries, Scotland;
Dec. 1844–Dec. 1863

**Question:** How did affected writers, patient printers, a later professional printer,
physicians, managers, commissioners, and law shape institutional authority, class separation,
work, and publication before and after the 1857 Scottish lunacy-law transition?

## Frozen corpus

Use only the `DGH1` archive plus the 1857 Royal Commission report and enacted Scottish Lunacy
Act. Predeclare six complete *New Moon* issue strata (inaugural; 1846–48; around the 1849
private/pauper separation; 1856–57; first complete post-Act issue; 1862–63), capture all
contributions in those issues, then select a declared 24–30-item microcorpus. Pair it with 1844,
1849, 1857, and 1862–63 annual-report strata; twelve internally coded private/pauper,
women/men, pre/post-1849 case records; the staff engagement register; and Adam Richardson's
1862–63 printing/employment correspondence.

## Required controls

- *New Moon* contains affected-person writing but is not transparent, freely expressive,
  representative, or entirely patient-controlled. Record issue, item, attributed writer,
  pseudonymity, genre, editor/selection evidence, print labor, image locator, and uncertainty.
- Separate patient contribution, patient printing, professional printing, superintendent,
  manager, trustee, commissioner, statute, casebook, staff register, and worker correspondence.
- Treat the 1849 private/pauper separation as a core class and institutional distinction. Do not
  narrate Crichton as one equal population.
- Richardson supplies one skilled-worker voice, not attendant, nursing, domestic, or farm-worker
  experience. Missing ordinary worker voice remains `Unknown`.
- Statute and commission recommendations do not establish immediate implementation. Institutional
  statistics and discharge labels do not establish objective diagnosis, prevalence, recovery,
  or effectiveness.
- Use internal case codes; suppress public names, addresses, family allegations, images, and
  easily re-identifying case references. Replay OCR/manuscript claims against images.
- Wellcome CC BY-NC and Dumfries reproduction controls require commercial-use review; link-only is
  the default. Privacy, dignity, self-harm, restraint, coercion, and historical-language review
  remain public gates.

## Exclusions and stop conditions

Exclude a national Scottish history, English/Irish/colonial comparison, post-1863 associations,
late-century eugenics, architecture survey, general nursing-professionalization claims, modern
art-therapy equivalence, treatment efficacy, retrospective diagnosis, and all “first asylum
periodical” claims. Stop any issue, case, worker, or implementation claim that cannot be replayed
against the exact archive image and dependency chain. No public prose or images.
