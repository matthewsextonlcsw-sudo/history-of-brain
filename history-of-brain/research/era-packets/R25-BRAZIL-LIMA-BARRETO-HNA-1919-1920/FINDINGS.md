# R25 Brazil: Lima Barreto at the HNA findings

## Packet disposition

**CONDITIONAL INTERNAL GO for the autograph-writing and transmission lane.** The complete diary
and novel manuscripts are open and reproducible as research objects. **HOLD** the institutional
counter-record and newspaper-interview lanes. No public prose, quotation display, translation,
facsimile or medical image is approved.

## What the opened sources establish

### A direct but situated affected-person manuscript

The first substantive leaf of the FBN diary scan begins in Barreto's first person. He locates
himself across institutional departments since Dec. 25 and describes entry through the
observation pavilion under police mediation (`EVD-037002`, `EVD-037003`). This is direct evidence
of what he wrote. It is not a universal account of HNA, independent proof of every event, a
clinical finding or a modern diagnosis.

The object is not a frictionless transcript. Faint pencil, corrections, narrow or reused paper,
later archive markings and attached notes remain visible (`EVD-037004`, `EVD-037009`,
`EVD-037010`). The complete scan and exact FBN description make a paleographic replay possible,
but the project has not approved a public transcription or translation.

### Fiction is a transformation layer, not corroboration

The second manuscript is a materially distinct literary object. Its first page opens with an
epigraph and a descriptive narrative about Rio rather than the diary's institutional first
person (`EVD-037006`, `EVD-037007`). Birman explicitly treats the unfinished novel as written
from diary material (`EVD-037014`). Because both works share author, experience and textual
development, agreement between them cannot be counted as independent confirmation.

### Institutional framing is mapped but not open

Birman identifies 1914 and 1919 observation-book records and reports that the 1919 record is a
one-page institutional entry (`EVD-037015`, `EVD-037016`, `EVD-037018`). Her article is a valid
source for her method and source map. It is not a substitute for directly opening the record.
The record's diagnostic heading, photograph and intake fields remain unusable here.

Birman's central methodological warning is usable: image, label and record frame must be
historicized rather than made to confirm one another automatically (`EVD-037017`). This does
not create a symmetrical “two sides” story. The diary and record were produced for different
purposes and under radically different power conditions.

### Archive and publication are part of the evidence chain

FBN reports acquisition of 1,134 archive documents from relatives in 1947 and later
digitization of the collection (`EVD-037011`, `EVD-037012`). The inventory binds each manuscript
to a shelfmark and digital object (`EVD-037008`–`EVD-037010`). These facts establish provenance
and access, not consent by relatives for every reuse or independence from later cataloging.

FBN also describes posthumous publication work associated with Francisco de Assis Barbosa
(`EVD-037013`). Any 1950s edition or modern transcript is therefore a separate source layer;
none may be silently treated as the autograph.

### Rights and dignity remain separate gates

The current FBN reproduction page requires conservation/copyright review and a responsibility
form for supplied images (`EVD-037019`). Brazilian law supplies a preliminary life-plus-70 term
screen for Barreto's authorial text (`EVD-037020`), but it does not settle FBN facsimile use,
modern editorial matter, translation rights, privacy, dignity, moral integrity or commercial
use. The medical portrait is rejected by default for this packet.
