## Publication and editorial mediation gate

For each future journal item, record separately:

- physical page and issue sequence;
- printed name, initials, pseudonym, anonymity or illegibility without identity expansion;
- writer, speaker, amanuensis, typist, editor, committee, printer and authorizing clinician;
- voluntary submission, prompting, assignment, dictation, copying, reported speech or unknown;
- language and script of the submitted, printed and translated layers;
- omission, correction, censorship, refusal, layout, later montage and copy-generation evidence;
- hospital circulation rule, audience, price/subscription statement and survival route; and
- what the text can establish about the speaker's report versus what it cannot establish about
  clinical cause, prevalence, institutional truth or outcome.

No fluency, gratitude, criticism, religious reference, handwriting, capitalization, length or
editorial placement may be used to infer a modern diagnosis, capacity, sincerity, recovery or
coercion.

## Photocopy, montage and archive-power gate

Marquis reports photocopies, missing originals, partial illegibility and a likely later header
montage. If acquired, every issue must receive:

1. repository-supplied identity and issue/date binding;
2. image order, recto/verso, blank, wrapper, fold, insertion and missing-page fields;
3. copy-generation, crop, contrast, skew, edge-loss and legibility notes;
4. a comparison of masthead, date, number, pagination, typography and later alterations;
5. raw-file checksums plus transformation lineage for any derivative; and
6. a second reader's check of pivotal transcriptions and all no. 1/no. 13 metadata.

OCR may assist navigation after visual replay. It may not reconstruct missing words, separate
authors, decide language, repair montage or become an independent source.

## Privacy, dignity, image and harm gates

- Preserve printed initials or pseudonyms; do not expand identities from other records.
- Do not publish a searchable roster, face, signature, ward/bed identifier, named medical case,

- Historical age, institutional publication, reading-room access, open metadata or copyright

- Any public use requires Algerian/postcolonial, disability/survivor, privacy, dignity,

## Representativeness ceiling

The fourteen issue-units are a purposive change-point acquisition sample. Even after complete
replay they cannot, without a separately approved design, establish:

- how representative published contributors were of hospital patients or workers;
- how common any activity, belief, request, criticism, treatment or outcome was;
- hospital-wide or Algerian practice;
- efficacy, recovery, discharge, voluntariness, consent, safety or diagnostic validity;
- the experiences of people unable or unwilling to write in French;
- family, community or subordinate-worker experience in general; or
- that surviving, selected and legible articles represent unpublished speech or institutional
  dissent.
