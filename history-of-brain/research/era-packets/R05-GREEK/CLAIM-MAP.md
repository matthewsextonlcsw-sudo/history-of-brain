## Claim-chain controls

- `CLM-006001`, `CLM-006002`, `CLM-006004`, and `CLM-006019` all ultimately depend on the
  same *Sacred Disease* textual tradition. The modern sources add interpretation, not four
  independent ancient witnesses.
- `CLM-006009` is a source-layer reconstruction transmitted here through Barrenechea's explicit
  citation of LiDonnici; those two mentions are one chain, not corroboration. `CLM-006021` is the
  distinct mediation proposition supported by Barrenechea's own analysis. `CLM-006010`,
  `CLM-006011`, `CLM-006018`, and `CLM-006021` still draw on the same surviving Epidaurian stelai;
  independent interpretations do not prove that a reported cure occurred.
- `CLM-006006` depends on later testimonia. Repetition in reference works cannot transform that
  chain into Alcmaeon's autograph or establish a global “first.”
- `CLM-006016` is a reception-history claim. It remains internal-outline only until a specialist
  reception history independent of Castelli's citation chain is opened; it should correct a
  popular origin myth without becoming a new totalizing claim that nothing changed.
