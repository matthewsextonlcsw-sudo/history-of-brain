## Source design

The packet separates seven layers: enacted Japanese law; administrative implementation
instructions; Japanese-government self-description; aggregate administrative counts; one
1959 Kanagawa administrative subset analyzed by later specialists; later scholarly
reconstruction of 1964–65 family organizing; and unopened acquisition targets. The English
1962 report is a Japanese Ministry of Health and Welfare publication inside a WHO-hosted
compilation, not an independent WHO evaluation or an authoritative translation of the 1950
law. No authoritative contemporaneous English translation of the full statute was found in
the bounded search; consequential translations therefore remain unapproved.
