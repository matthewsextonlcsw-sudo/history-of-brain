# R31 Japan Mental Hygiene Law, 1950–1965 — findings

**Scope:** Japan national mental-hygiene administration, with a bounded 1959 Kanagawa
Article 23 application-file lens, 1950-05-01 through 1965-10-01.

**Question:** Did consultation and visiting-guidance provisions shift care beyond hospitals,
or did admission, finance, and protector duties continue to channel responsibility through
families and institutions?
