## Observation / inference controls

- Unguja occupation layers and structures are **observations**; mosque relationship, ceremonial-center function and bead protection are **inferences**.
- Later oral testimony and lexical reconstruction are evidence about remembered history and language change; they are not automatic eyewitness reports of sixteenth-century events.
- `Uganga` is retained as a locally grounded, semantically broad term. It is not normalized to medicine, psychology, psychiatry or spirituality.
- *Vitio* is a source category associated in later records with bodily symptoms and social/moral context. It is not mapped to a psychiatric diagnosis.
- *Tambuka* author, commissioner and 1728 statements are transmission claims. The claimed 1728 manuscript is disputed and missing; surviving witness/edition counts vary.
- An inscribed object called an amulet in an inaccessible abstract is not evidence of a treatment, wearer belief, illness or outcome.
