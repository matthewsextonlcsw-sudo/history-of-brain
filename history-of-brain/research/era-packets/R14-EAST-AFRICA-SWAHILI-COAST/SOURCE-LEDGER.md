## Bounded corpus and question

This packet compares three archives without inventing a unitary regional system:

1. **Unguja Ukuu, Zanzibar:** controlled archaeology and an inaccessible late-eighth-/ninth-century rock-crystal seal/amulet study.
2. **Mombasa's Mijikenda-speaking hinterland:** a sixteenth–seventeenth-century question approached through archaeology, historical linguistics, and explicitly later oral/ethnographic records.
3. **Pate/Lamu:** the *Utendi wa Tambuka* attribution, witness, edition, and collector chain; literary/archive history only.

The question is what these different archives can establish about protection, healing knowledge, household or community response to adversity, and what they cannot establish about distress, diagnosis, treatment, experience, or outcomes before 1800.
