## What the packet can carry into an outline

1. **An archive-method transition:** material remains, later oral/linguistic reconstruction and manuscript transmission answer different questions; none is a direct substitute for patient testimony.
2. **Unguja settlement context:** controlled archaeology supports a substantial early settlement and a probable mosque hypothesis with explicit unresolved date/function tests.
3. **A negative object finding:** the rock-crystal seal/amulet story is not publication-ready because full source, exact context, object record, custody and rights were not obtained.
4. **Mombasa terminology:** use Mijikenda-speaking communities for earlier periods and explain the later ethnonym seam.
5. **Locally grounded concepts without equivalence:** uganga has a wider semantic range than “medicine”; later vitio/gophu/vifudu evidence must not be psychiatricized or back-projected whole.
6. **Later narrators as thinkers:** Thomas Govi should be credited as a historiologist, with the Spear translation/audio mediation and 1971 collection date visible.
7. **Living authority:** current kaya councils/individual authorities are protocol principals; UNESCO/State Party language is a cue, not proxy permission.
8. **Pate/Lamu transmission:** author/commission/1728 claims are traditions; the 1728 witness claim is disputed and the manuscript reported missing; later witnesses and editions vary.
9. **Archive labor:** coastal poets, scribes and interpreters materially made the collections now attributed to European scholars and repositories.
10. **A bounded lived-experience finding:** the opened packet does not contain direct pre-1800 affected-person accounts of distress, care, coercion, treatment or outcome.

## Myths and both-sides audit

| Tempting story | Evidence-based correction | Opposite overcorrection to avoid |
|---|---|---|
| An amulet proves treatment | Object function, wearer intention, illness and outcome are unverified | Do not claim objects had no protective meaning |
| Spirit language equals mental illness | Source categories and modern diagnoses are not equivalent | Do not erase religious or ancestral registers |
| Uganga means medicine | The term spans healing and multiple other forms of specialized power/knowledge | Do not make it an ineffable timeless spirituality |
| Community care was universal and humane | Later sources show guarded knowledge, fees, hierarchy, accusation and possible coercion | Do not infer that care or mutual aid was absent |
| Oral tradition is either fact or myth | Later narrators are theorists; traditions have collection, performance and translation histories | Do not strip narrators of historical expertise |
| European catalogs preserve the original literature | Catalogs preserve mediated, often commissioned copies and variable texts | Do not imply commissioned copies lack local intellectual value |
| *Tambuka* was written in 1728 | 1728 is a disputed transmission/witness claim; the alleged manuscript is missing | Do not claim the poem cannot be early eighteenth century |
| No patient voice means no mental-health history | It means this packet cannot recover direct affected experience | Do not invent feelings to fill the gap |
