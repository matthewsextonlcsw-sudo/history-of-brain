# R32 WHO — Expert Authority and Mental Health Recommendations, 1948–1955

**Status:** Conditional internal institutional-authority GO; public prose prohibited

**Place and dates:** WHO/WHA and four Geneva expert sessions, June 24, 1948–Nov. 1955; expert
meetings Aug. 29, 1949–Nov. 6, 1954.

**Question:** How did WHO's rotating “nuclear” Expert Committee turn postwar mental hygiene into
recommendations about public-health practice, community psychiatric services, diagnostic
standardization, and psychiatric law—and whose professional and geographic authority was
present or missing?

## Frozen corpus

Use the exact WHA authority record; complete TRS 9, 31, 73, and 98 reports; and tightly bounded
1950/1951 WHO work reports. Build a membership/profession/geography/voice matrix and a separate
English/French/Spanish edition map. Underlying WHO/Ment mimeographs remain acquisition gates for
any claim about debate, authorship, or revision. National implementation requires a separate
country evidence object and is outside this packet.

## Controls and stops

Committee reports describe collective expert views and explicitly do not equal WHO decisions,
binding standards, country delivery, or outcomes. Record affiliations without treating experts
as national delegates. Make the absence of patient, family, nurse, social-worker, aide, labor,
and Indigenous/traditional-healer members visible. Treat colonial and diagnostic language as an
object of analysis, not project vocabulary or epidemiologic fact. Cross-language claims require
paired full editions, page locators, and qualified review. Pre-2017 WHO scans are not assumed
Creative Commons; no cover, table, scan, long excerpt, house translation, emblem, or implied
endorsement. No “WHO invented,” global consensus, community-deinstitutionalization, modern-
rights, or patient-experience claim.
