5. Period colonial, racialized, gendered, and diagnostic language is evidence about the report,

## Bounded findings

- The exact authority record is WHA2.25's act of noting the 1949 convening, not blanket adoption
  of four reports.
- The committee's program widened from priorities/classification, through preventive public
  health, to hospital/community services and psychiatric-law recommendations.
- Across 32 member-seats, affiliation geography is Europe 17, Americas 9, Asia 4, Africa 1,
  Oceania 1. This is a seat count across sessions, not unique-person, citizenship, population,
  or representational legitimacy data.
- Two member-seats are clearly identified as women by printed name/honorific: Elsa B. Nordlund
  and Mme J. Roudinesco. This is not self-identified gender data.
- Professional medical/psychiatric authority dominated. Mentioned patients, families, nurses,
  social workers and former-patient clubs do not become participating voices.
- The corpus provides no direct patient, family, worker, nurse, social-worker, aide, legal-
  profession, Indigenous, or traditional-healer testimony.
- French and Spanish titles, imprints, months, and pagination are independently documented;
  semantic equivalence and working language remain unresolved.
