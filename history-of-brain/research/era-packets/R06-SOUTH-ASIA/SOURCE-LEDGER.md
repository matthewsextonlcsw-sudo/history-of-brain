## Retrieval, scope, and rating note

The named core is **“Five textual laboratories of mind, suffering, disturbance, and cultivation in northern and peninsular South Asia, within the brief's broad first-millennium-BCE to first-millennium-CE horizon.”** It samples: selected early Upaniṣadic passages; early Buddhist Pāli discourses and the *Therīgāthā*; Jain *Tattvārthasūtra* materials; the *Carakasaṃhitā* on *unmāda*; and the *Pātañjalayogaśāstra*. The packet did not establish a common `500 BCE–500 CE` range for these unlike corpora. Corpus-specific composition, redaction, manuscript, edition, and translation dates remain unresolved except where a claim explicitly attributes a bounded reconstruction to a named scholar. It is not a history of a timeless “Indian mind.”

Agent Reach was checked before retrieval. `agent-reach doctor --json` reported Exa unavailable and the web route available through **Jina Reader**. Full webpages were opened with Jina; discovery used the authorized built-in primary-source web-search fallback. Search snippets, abstracts, publisher blurbs, and generated summaries were leads only. Every retained factual proposition below comes from an opened complete edition, complete primary-text page/chapter, or complete specialist study.

Ratings are relationship-specific: `L1–L5/LX` is source level; `A–D` is fit to the stated job. A text or translation can be L1-A for what that witness says and D for actual prevalence, patient experience, efficacy, or equivalence to a modern diagnosis.

## Source-chain controls

- `SRC-009011`, `SRC-009012`, and `SRC-009013` are witnesses to one *Carakasaṃhitā* tradition, not independent corroboration. `SRC-009002` and `SRC-009014` are later interpretations that ultimately cite versions of that tradition.
- `SRC-009006–SRC-009009` share Bilara/SuttaCentral infrastructure and Pāli canonical transmission. Separate discourses are distinct texts, but not independent proof of historical speech or practice.
- `SRC-009001` and `SRC-009004` overlap on the PYŚ. Maas supplies historical-critical context; Woods supplies an old edition/translation.
- `SRC-009009` preserves a canonical verse-voice; `SRC-009010` and `SRC-009015` qualify how voice, attribution, and oral transmission should be described. They do not authenticate an autobiography.
- `SRC-009016` is a discovery lead, not retained substantive support. Its pages may identify passages for follow-up, but no claim about ancient training, disability/status categories, exclusion, women/guardians, access, or implementation may rest on the anonymous translation.
