## Cross-packet handoffs

### To every prehistoric/material-evidence packet

Apply the inference ladder in `FINDINGS.md` and record the highest rung reached. In particular:

- record object/tomb ID and preservation before diagnosis;
- separate deliberate alteration from medical indication;
- separate healing from successful treatment;
- separate impairment from disability/dependence;
- separate assistance from care regime;
- stop before motive, compassion, stigma, diagnosis of mind, or subjective experience without direct evidence.

### To R02-MESOPOTAMIA, R03-EGYPT, R03-NUBIA-KUSH, and R04-LEVANT

When written sources exist, do not let them retroactively explain prehistoric material evidence. A later medical/religious text can document its own period and category; it cannot establish M487's motive or the belief behind an earlier cranial opening. Build an explicit temporal/independence bridge or keep the cases separate.

### To ancient written-world packet researchers

Use this packet as the negative control for patient voice. A practitioner text is not automatically a patient's account. When a written source names symptoms, possession, madness, spirit action, or treatment, identify speaker, recorder, institutional purpose, genre, translation, and coercion before treating it as lived experience.

### To the timeline coordinator

- M487 may be a node about **how archaeologists distinguish deliberate cranial opening from inferred medicine**, not “first brain surgery.”
- M9 may be a node about **survival with profound impairment and the evidence limits of care reconstruction**, with date range clearly identified as cemetery chronology.
- Shanidar 1 should remain **Disputed** on necessary care/incapacity until `SRC-001006` is fully reviewed.
- Do not put “first compassion,” “first healthcare,” or “first surgery” in timeline labels.

### To future SEO/GEO research

The public misconceptions documented here are search opportunities only after source review:

- “Was trepanation brain surgery?”
- “How do archaeologists know someone survived trepanation?”
- “Did Neanderthals care for disabled people?”

The answer architecture must preserve separate rungs and cannot optimize by collapsing uncertainty into a yes/no hook.
