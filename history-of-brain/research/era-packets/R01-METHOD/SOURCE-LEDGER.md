## Independence map

- `IG-M487-DIRECT`: one original case-analysis chain. `SRC-001001` independently informs method but does not independently observe M487.
- `IG-MANBAC-M9-DIRECT`: `SRC-001002` and `SRC-001003` are one evidence chain with overlapping authors and the same specimen. They are not two-source corroboration.
- `IG-SHANIDAR1-AUDITORY-2017`: `SRC-001005` reports an auditory examination performed in 1976–78 and published in 2017; this is the case-specific source chain for the exostoses and inferred hearing effects.
- `IG-SHANIDAR1-INJURY-INHERITED`: `SRC-001005` S1 synthesizes/reanalyzes the older Stewart/Trinkaus injury-analysis chain. It is not independent corroboration of that inherited chain, and repeated public accounts do not add observations.
- `IG-PUBLIC-SHANIDAR-SOLECKI`: `SRC-001009`, `SRC-001010`, and `SRC-001012` are public descendants of the same Shanidar interpretive tradition. Agreement among them is not corroboration.
- `IG-CARE-CRITIQUE-DETTWYLER`, `IG-CARE-DEBATE-SYNTHESIS`, and `IG-DISABILITY-EXPERTISE-DAN` are distinct argumentative routes, but only the first two directly adjudicate prehistoric care inference.

## Source-level rejections and non-uses

- Search snippets, Wikipedia, Reddit, generic “prehistoric medicine” pages, and AI-generated summaries were discovery leads only and received no IDs because no retained claim depends on them.
- `SRC-001006` is preserved as `LX` rather than silently omitted because its full disability-led reanalysis is likely pivotal for the next review. Its abstract may guide acquisition; it does not verify the advertised conclusion.
- No modern diagnosis, treatment advice, prevalence estimate, or claim about prehistoric mental illness was derived from this packet.
