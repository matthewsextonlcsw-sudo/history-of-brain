# R01-METHOD — Reading Minds from Material Evidence

**State:** Authorized; research only

**Owner:** One research session; this folder only

**Identifier range:** `SRC/EVD/CLM-001000–001999`

## Research question

What can material evidence responsibly establish about injury, intervention, survival,
disability, assistance, and care before direct written testimony—and where must claims about
mind, motive, ritual, diagnosis, stigma, or compassion stop?

## Boundary

This is a **method-and-case calibration packet**, not a world survey of prehistory. Use a small,
explicitly justified set of cases representing different evidence problems, including
trepanation and survival with impairment. Dates and places belong to each case. Prefer cases
with accessible excavation/context reports and active scholarly disagreement.

## In scope

- dating and archaeological context;
- physical observation versus behavioral and belief inference;
- paleopathology and bioarchaeological care arguments;
- survival, impairment, assistance, and alternative explanations;
- how museums and popular accounts overstate prehistoric “medicine” or “compassion”;
- ethical language around human remains and disability; and
- a reusable inference ladder for later packets.

## Out of scope

- a global catalog of trepanned skulls;
- retrospective psychiatric diagnosis;
- claims about a society's entire religion or emotional life;
- later written medical traditions; and
- public article prose.

## Required evidence targets

- original excavation, osteological, dating, or object records for every core case;
- specialist peer-reviewed methodological scholarship;
- at least one serious critique of “bioarchaeology of care” inference;
- disability-history or disability-bioarchaeology scholarship;
- repository/rights/protocol information for any remains or images considered; and
- examples of downstream claims whose citation chains succeed or fail.

No patient voice survives. The archive file must state this plainly rather than invent an
experience analogue.

## Required outputs

Create `SOURCE-LEDGER.md`, `FINDINGS.md`, `CLAIM-MAP.md`,
`LIVED-EXPERIENCE-AND-ARCHIVE.md`, and `GAPS-AND-HANDOFF.md` in this folder. Save lawful source
files under `../../source-cache/R01-METHOD/` and record rights plus SHA-256 in the ledger.

## Stop condition

Stop after the bounded cases support a tested inference ladder and a reproducible source chain.
Do not expand into a comprehensive history of prehistoric healing and do not write the public
page.
