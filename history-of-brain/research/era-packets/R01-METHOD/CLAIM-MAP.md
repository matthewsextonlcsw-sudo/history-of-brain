## Contradiction records for coordinator synthesis

### `DIS-001000` — Does long survival with impairment prove compassion?

- **Position A:** some Shanidar/M9 literature and public pages treat survival and help as evidence of compassion or humanity (`SRC-001003`, `001005`, `001009`, `001010`).
- **Position B:** Dettwyler argues bones cannot establish treatment, motive, quality of life, productivity, or morality (`SRC-001004`).
- **Resolution for this packet:** distinguish assistance/care behavior from compassionate motive. Some assistance may be inferable; compassion remains unknown. Public language must not make moral worth the test of “humanness.”

### `DIS-001001` — Was Shanidar 1 incapacitated and necessarily cared for?

- **Support case:** cumulative lesion/function argument in `SRC-001005`.
- **Qualification:** ability/productivity critique in `SRC-001004`; disabled people adapt and contribute.
- **New contradiction not yet verified:** `SRC-001006` advertises an activity/disability-expertise reanalysis rejecting necessary incapacitation, but full text is restricted.
- **Resolution:** keep `CLM-001013` Disputed; acquire and fully review `SRC-001006` before public wording.

### `DIS-001002` — What caused Shanidar 1's right-arm condition?

- **Current direct differential:** distal humeral fractures, non-union/atrophy, later forearm/hand loss or amputation (`SRC-001005`, S1 pp. 3–4).
- **Institutional public page:** head injury damaged brain areas and led to withered arm/possible paralysis (`SRC-001012`).
- **Resolution:** public institutional account is not compatible with the current direct analysis and should not be repeated without tracing an independent basis.

### `DIS-001003` — Was M487 medical or ritual trepanation?

- **Medical possibility:** attempted decompression/treatment near death.
- **Postmortem possibility:** ritual/magical opening.
- **Shared problem:** no observed antecedent trauma, no healing, one case, no direct tradition/testimony.
- **Resolution:** exact motive Unknown; use M487 to teach equifinality, not prehistoric medical belief.
