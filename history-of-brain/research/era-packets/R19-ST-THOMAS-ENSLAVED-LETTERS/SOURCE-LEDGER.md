## Bounded design and access route

The historical corpus is six documents from St. Thomas, 1738–1753, in Unity Archives Herrnhut record group `R.15.B.a`, consulted through Cefas van Rossem and Hein van der Voort's complete relevant diplomatic-edition chapter, *Die Creol taal* (1996), pp. 49–86. The original manuscripts were not available as public online facsimiles in the bounded search. Item records below therefore distinguish the archival item from the consulted edition, and no handwriting, deletion, seal, or physical-layout claim is made from the edition.

`agent-reach doctor --json` reported Agent Reach v1.5.0 and Jina/Web available. Exa was unavailable because its MCP route was not configured. Jina, direct institutional pages, complete PDFs, and primary/scholarly web-search fallback were used. Search snippets and abstracts were leads only. The full 1996 edition, complete 2017 dissertation, complete 2024 Bøegh chapter, complete 2025 Gerbner monograph, complete 2025 Bøegh article, complete Odumosu article, and all cited institutional/community pages were opened.
