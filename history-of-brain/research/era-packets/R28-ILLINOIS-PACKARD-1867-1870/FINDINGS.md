# R28 Illinois Packard counterpublic — findings and evidence

**State:** source-opened internal findings; no public prose

## Evidence summary

The 1868 volume is materially composite. Packard is author and publisher of the first book and
editor-publisher of the separately titled Olsen narrative and appended testimony. Olsen is a
named author, but Packard adds a prefatory explanation that overrides or supplements what Olsen
withheld. The four named testimony sections carry separate speaker positions while remaining
inside Packard's selected publication. The 1867 committee is a state investigation with private
sessions, credibility rules, and administrative power; it is neither a neutral transcript nor
merely Packard's corroborator. The statute establishes legal design. The later Board report
shows that commitment procedure remained contested and institutionally interpreted.
