## Method and search record

- The packet follows `EVIDENCE-MODEL.md` and `SOURCE-RATING-AND-CAPTURE-PROTOCOL.md`. Ratings are claim-relative: L1 direct/authoritative evidence, L2 specialist scholarship, L3 authoritative reference, L4 accountable secondary account, L5 discovery lead, LX rejected/unusable; fit A direct, B strong indirect, C context, D mismatch.
- Agent Reach health check found Jina Reader active and Exa unavailable. Full reachable pages and PDFs were opened through Jina or directly. Built-in primary-source web search was used as the required discovery fallback. Search snippets, abstracts, publisher descriptions, and tables of contents were treated only as leads.
- Key popular claims were traced upstream. A downstream statement is not counted as independent corroboration when it repeats Herodotus, Breasted 1930, Bryan 1930, or another shared source.

## Rejected or not-yet-opened leads

- Search snippets, Wikipedia, Crystalinks, SEO medical-history pages, Reddit reposts, and AI summaries are not evidence. They were used only to identify claims and upstream citations.
- Sanchez and Meltzer’s Case 6 chapter (`SRC-003003`), Wade and Nelson (`SRC-003013`), and Clayton (`SRC-003024`) remain unusable for substantive claims until the full relevant text is lawfully opened.
