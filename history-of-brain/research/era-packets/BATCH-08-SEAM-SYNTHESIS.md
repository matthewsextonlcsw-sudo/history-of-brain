## Packet assembly decisions

### 1. Japan shows a dual legal-administrative architecture, not demonstrated deinstitutionalization

The 1950 law combined prefectural hospital provision and multiple non-patient-consent routes
with optional consultation offices and visiting guidance. It assigned duties to a legally
designated protector, including financial and discharge-retrieval obligations, and included a
temporary home-restraint route. Government reports later described congestion, hospital and bed
growth, surveys, centres, consultation, visits, and outpatient activity. The 1965 amendment
expanded public-health-centre, mental-hygiene-centre, and outpatient-support design while also
expanding police-notification and emergency-admission powers and repealing the home-restraint
route.

The safe internal conclusion is coexistence and expansion in both directions. The opened record
does not show consistent local staffing or delivery, reduced institutionalization, better
outcomes, voluntariness, or whether people experienced consultation and visits as assistance,
surveillance, or transferred family burden. The 1959 Kanagawa Article 23 material is a bounded
aggregate administrative lens on 154 application records, not a case-history corpus or national
sample. The 1962 English chapter is a Japanese-government report hosted by WHO, not an
independent WHO evaluation or authoritative English legal translation.

### 2. WHO expert reports document professional recommendation-making, not world policy

WHA2.25 records that the Assembly noted the Director-General's action in convening a 1949
committee. It does not adopt the four later reports as a unit. The opened reports show a widening
professional agenda from priorities and classification, through preventive public health, to a
hospital-centred community-service model and psychiatric-law recommendations. Their own status
language distinguishes collective expert views from WHO decisions or stated policy.

The four rosters contain 32 session-seats: 17 European affiliations, 9 Americas, 4 Asia,
1 Africa, and 1 Oceania. These are repeated seats and printed affiliations, not unique people,
citizenship, delegation, population weighting, or legitimate global representation. Medical and
psychiatric authority dominated. Patients, former patients, families, nurses, social workers,
aides, labor, lawyers, Indigenous authorities, and traditional healers may be discussed, but
they do not speak directly in the opened corpus. A proposed former-patient club remains an
expert-mediated recommendation, not patient authorship, operation, authority transfer, or
outcome evidence.

### 3. Aro supplies bounded program descriptions while preserving organizer dependence

villages. They also show incomplete hospital facilities and retained asylum lanes. A successor
institution's later 1956 date remains a separate chronology position rather than a silent

The BMJ article, the UN summary, and underlying unopened F/121 paper belong to one

recovery, community tolerance, and larger-scale adoption remain attributed organizer rhetoric,

exchange. Government permission to use hospital material is not participant consent, privacy
clearance, copyright permission, or present reuse authority.
