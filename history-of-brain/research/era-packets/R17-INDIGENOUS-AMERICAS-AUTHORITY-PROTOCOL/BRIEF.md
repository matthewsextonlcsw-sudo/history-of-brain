# R17-INDIGENOUS-AMERICAS-AUTHORITY-PROTOCOL — Who May Authorize the Research?

**State:** Authorized for governance/protocol research only; no cultural history authorized

**Identifier range:** `029000–029999`

## Research question

Under whose authority, using which records, agreements, permissions, restrictions, and review
mechanisms may a later Indigenous Americas research packet begin?

## Boundary

This is not a pan-Indigenous history and does not select communities to fill a coverage matrix.
It maps jurisdiction-first authority paths and project actions. CARE may supply a cross-cutting
governance framework, but community-specific rules control. OCAP® is specific to First Nations;
it is not automatically an Inuit, Métis, US Tribal, Native Hawaiian, Latin American, Caribbean,
or universal Indigenous framework.

## Required decision process

For every proposed future corpus:

1. identify the exact object, record, recording, site, collection, language, date layer, holder,
   and proposed use;
2. identify present-day cultural affiliation, including disputed/shared/unknown affiliation;
3. identify the relevant Nation, people, community, government, research body, archive,
   cultural-preservation office, or other authorized decision maker;
4. check human-remains, funerary, sacred, cultural-patrimony, sensitive-site, language,
   recording, family/privacy, medicinal-knowledge, and other special-material rules;
5. record an agreement governing reading, extraction, storage, quotation, translation, image or
   audio reuse, public indexing, GEO/AI discoverability, commercial context, attribution,
   benefit/return, community review, updates, withdrawal, and expiration; and
6. issue only a controlled gate: `AUTHORIZED_FOR_SCOPING`,
   `AUTHORIZED_FOR_INTERNAL_EXTRACTION`, `AUTHORIZED_FOR_PUBLIC_DRAFT`, `LINK_ONLY`,
   `RESTRICTED`, `AUTHORITY_UNRESOLVED`, or `DO_NOT_USE`.

## Evidence-layer taxonomy

Create separate requirements for material/archaeological evidence; community-authorized oral
knowledge/history; later-recorded tradition; colonial/mission/administrative/ethnographic
records; modern Indigenous-governed interpretation; and modern specialist synthesis.
Repository metadata and specialist scholarship cannot confer community authority.

## Default non-reproduction classes

Until the relevant authority clears the intended use, do not cache, quote extensively,
transcribe, translate, illustrate, or reproduce human remains/burials, funerary belongings,
sacred or restricted knowledge, songs/prayers/ritual instructions, recordings/voices, sensitive
locations, deceased-person material subject to restriction, inadequately consented community
knowledge, identifying family/health/school records, biocultural/genetic/specimen information,
medicinal knowledge, or community-customized Local Contexts metadata.

The project may use a Local Contexts Notice while engagement is unresolved. It must never
self-assign a community's TK or BC Label.

## Required future-lane audit

Map, without authorizing by default, jurisdiction-first paths for US Tribal Nations, Alaska
Native communities, Native Hawaiian organizations, First Nations, Inuit regions/communities,
Métis governments/communities, and named peoples/communities in Mexico, Central America, the
Andes, Amazonia, Southern Cone, and Caribbean. Preserve the scoped Nahua central-Mexico
1519–1606 proposal as `AUTHORITY_UNRESOLVED` unless this packet establishes its actual authority
path and allowed actions.

## Stop

The packet is complete when every proposed lane has a named authority path and allowed actions,
or is explicitly held. Stop when affiliation is disputed, authority unclear, all affected
communities have not been addressed, permission comes only from a repository, translation
authority is absent, public SEO/AI indexing was not disclosed, restricted material appears, or
a community requests a pause. No public prose.
