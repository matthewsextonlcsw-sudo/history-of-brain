## Method and boundary

This ledger answers only who can authorize later research, which framework applies, and what the
project must hold. It does not infer an authority from a cultural label, a repository, a nation-state,
or a broad regional name. A framework can identify duties without granting permission. Copyright,
repository access, research-ethics review, statutory compliance, and community authority are
separate gates.

Agent Reach v1.5.0 `doctor --json` was run on 2026-08-19. Jina Reader was available. Exa was
unavailable because `mcporter` and the Exa MCP backend were not configured. Full Jina pages,
direct official documents, and primary-source web search were used as the fallback. Abstracts and
search snippets were leads only. All sources below were opened by `R17 Codex protocol researcher`
on 2026-08-19.
