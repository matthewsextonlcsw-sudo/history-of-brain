# R33 Aro Village Scheme findings

## Packet finding

The opened corpus supports a bounded internal history of what organizers and medical
administrators described: an October 1954 day-hospital start, selected boarding in neighboring
villages, accompanying relatives, a nursing presence, outpatient treatment, occupational
activity, and later use of two villages. It does not support direct experience, consent, rent
terms, labor conditions, cost, comparative outcomes, recovery, voluntariness, stigma change,
or national transformation. BMJ 1956 and UN 1963 are one organizer chain, not two independent
confirmations.
