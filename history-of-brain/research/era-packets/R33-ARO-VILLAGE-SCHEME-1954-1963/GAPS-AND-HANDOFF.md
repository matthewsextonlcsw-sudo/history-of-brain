## Exact acquisition route

### 1. Applicable law

Acquire an official or authoritative complete copy of:

1. the Lunacy Ordinance, Cap.121, in the 1948 Laws of Nigeria version operative at the October
   1954 start, including forms, schedules, commencement and amendment history;
2. Western Nigeria's Lunacy Law/Cap.73 layer if a revised edition replaced or adapted Cap.121;
3. the Lunacy Ordinance (Amendment) Law No.16 of 1959, complete;
4. constitutional/adaptation instruments determining territorial and temporal application; and
5. regulations or orders specific to Aro/Lantoro if any.

Record enactment/commencement separately. Obtain Nigerian legal-historical review. Do not infer
that asylum certification applied to selected village-boarded patients, or that physical
placement resolves detention, custody, capacity or consent.

### 2. Lambo 1961 chapter

Acquire the complete pp.215-231 chapter in Louis Linn, ed., *Frontiers in General Hospital
Psychiatry*. Verify edition, pagination and copyright. Replay all pages and citations. Extract
rent, selection, family, staffing, labor, consent, refusal, safety and outcome statements only
if actually present; do not backfill from later quotations.

### 3. Conference and F/121

- Obtain the complete approximately 320-page *Conference Report: First Pan-African Psychiatric
  Conference*, including title pages, contents, participant list, every Aro contribution,
  discussion, appendices and editorial method.
- Obtain the exact UN paper F/121 by A.H. Leighton and T.A. Lambo. Collate it with pp.87-88 of
  the UN summary and record additions, omissions and editorial changes.
- Test whether any participant, family, villager, nurse or worker speaks directly. A professional
  participant list is not community voice.

### 4. Direct-voice and administrative routes

Search named, governed collections rather than general web summaries for:

- patient/survivor writing or oral history;
- accompanying-family letters, petitions, interviews or account books;
- host-household contracts, rent ledgers, disputes or village minutes;
- nursing reports, rosters, training records, union/workforce files or oral histories;
- occupational-therapy production, pay, market, refusal, injury or disciplinary records; and
- complaints, adverse events, court challenges or discharge/follow-up records.

Before opening clinical or named material, secure repository terms, privacy plan, dignity review
and a no-reidentification protocol. Absence after one search is not archive-wide absence.

### 5. Film

First identify title, date, creator, commissioner, holder, catalogue number, complete runtime,
language, soundtrack and every surviving version. Then obtain the full film and transcript
lawfully. Create shot-level provenance and mediation records. No patient face, voice, clinical
scene, family image or community image is approved by default. Require copyright, performer,
health-privacy, dignity, descendant/family/community-authority and necessity review.
