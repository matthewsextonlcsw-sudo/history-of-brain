## Why these packets belong together

Batch 04 exposed two different kinds of gap. Several African lanes had not yet passed even a
bounded source-feasibility gate, while Indigenous Americas work could not responsibly begin
without a jurisdiction- and community-specific authority process. At the same chronological
seam, European print, anatomy, self-writing, and consultation records required separation from
the medieval and later-asylum stories.

This batch therefore does not promise six comparable regional narratives. Three packets test
what African material, manuscript, oral, and later-recorded archives can responsibly support;
one creates a governance gate rather than cultural content; and two investigate distinct
early-modern European evidence surfaces. A gap, hold, or `AUTHORITY_UNRESOLVED` result is a
successful research result when the evidence or authority does not permit more.

## Search and source rules

- Agent Reach v1.5.0 was current at batch start. Jina Reader was available; Exa was unavailable
  because its MCP backend was not configured. Record this and use direct primary,
  community-governed, institutional, and specialist-source search fallback.
- Open the complete relevant source. Abstracts, snippets, catalogs, search summaries, and later
  retellings are leads unless the narrow metadata/reception claim is their actual job.
- Trace “first,” diagnosis, treatment, continuity, influence, institution, authorship,
  translation, and efficacy claims upstream.
- One evidence record normally names one source. Shared parent witnesses and dependent
  citation chains never count as independent corroboration.
- Separate object/site, composition, inscription, recording, copying, translation, edition,
  excavation, collection, digitization, custody, and reception dates.
- Preserve source language, consulted language, translator/editor, source position, and exact
  text/object/site locator.

## Cultural authority and rights rules

- Copyright, open access, repository access, research permission, and cultural authority are
  separate gates.
- Do not cache or reproduce sacred, ceremonial, secret, seasonal, initiation-restricted,
  funerary, human-remains, vulnerable-site, voice/recording, medicinal-knowledge, or
  community-controlled material without the relevant authority and intended-use permission.
- Repository metadata cannot grant community consent. A modern institution cannot stand in for
  a Nation, people, community, traditional council, Elders Council, custodian, or knowledge
  holder.
- Record public search indexing, GEO/AI discoverability, commercial context, translation,
  image, quotation, and withdrawal/update expectations as distinct proposed uses.
- Restricted, unclear-rights, private, or culturally controlled full text remains link-only.
  Rights-safe cache files live only in the ignored `research/source-cache/<packet-id>/` path
  with exact hashes and use limits in the ledger.

## Historical guardrails

- Do not create an “African psychology,” “San worldview,” “Indigenous belief system,” “European
  discovery,” or superstition-to-science narrative.
- Material evidence does not directly disclose diagnosis, belief, experience, ritual meaning,
  or treatment. Later oral or ethnographic analogy cannot silently supply those meanings.
- A prescription, prayer, image, literary voice, consultation entry, or manuscript does not
  prove implementation, access, consent, outcome, prevalence, or continuity.
- Translate historical terms cautiously. Do not make spirit, soul, heart, fear, melancholy,
  possession, protection, medicine, or passion equal a current diagnosis or profession.
- Name missing patients, recipients, households, women, workers, poor or dependent people,
  disabled people, translators, scribes, collectors, and community authorities.
- No public prose, quotation clearance, SEO/GEO copy, asset publication, implementation,
  deployment, or live claims.

cross-review.
