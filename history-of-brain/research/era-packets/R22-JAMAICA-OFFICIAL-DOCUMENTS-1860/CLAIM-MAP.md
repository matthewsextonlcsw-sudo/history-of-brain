## Myth and wording controls

- The separate *Seven Months* was not opened; the elicited statement is not a substitute.
- “Official” does not mean neutral, complete, accurate, representative, or ethically publishable.
- Historical medical labels do not settle credibility and do not map to modern diagnosis.
- Family traces do not prove abandonment, support, consent, or indifference.
- Worker testimony is neither automatically institutional propaganda nor transparent outsider
  observation; rank, dependency, elicitation, directness, and uncertainty must travel with it.
- A new inquiry, building, law, or officer does not prove improved patient experience.
- One Kingston dossier cannot stand for the Caribbean or colonial institutions generally.
