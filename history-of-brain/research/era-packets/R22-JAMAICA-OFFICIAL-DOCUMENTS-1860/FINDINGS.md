## Working findings

The opened object is not one voice and not a neutral inquiry transcript. It is an institutional
counter-dossier ordered by the governor, compiled by the institution's inspector/director, and
distributed to public officials and British readers. Within that frame it preserves differently
mediated patient statements, worker examinations, medical and administrative defenses, legal
documents, and reported family traces. Each evidence record below specifies who spoke, who
elicited or selected the material, and what the record cannot establish.

The dossier documents a contest over coercion, credibility, labor, food, access to doctors and
supporters, institutional discipline, historical illness categories, and public authority. It does
not resolve which allegation was true, supply a direct family-authored account, reproduce Ann
Pratt's separate pamphlet, establish the 1861 commission's findings, or permit prevalence,
diagnosis, efficacy, consent, outcome, reform-causation, or Caribbean-wide conclusions.
