## Myth and simplification quarantine

The following formulations are unsafe for an outline, headline, SEO page, reel, script, or image
prompt unless later evidence directly resolves them:

| unsafe formulation | why held | minimum repair |
|---|---|---|
| “Ann Pratt wrote exactly…” | her separate primary pamphlet was not opened; the hostile dossier is not a substitute | open and replay `CO 137/350/429–441`; resolve authorship and edition questions |
| “Pratt exposed the asylum and caused reform” | influence and causation require the full later archive and a broader design | open testimony/report, administrative follow-through, and Jamaican/metropolitan reception records |
| “The commission proved/vindicated…” | no commission primary volume was opened | open `CO 137/359–364`; record item-level evidence, disagreement, and limits |
| “A new asylum made care humane” | a building/policy claim is not an outcome measure | obtain operational and patient-experience evidence after the change |

| “We hear the patients in their own words” | statements were elicited, recorded, selected, and printed; some people appear only through reported speech | name the procedure and mediation for each voice |
| “Her family abandoned her” | one warrant conflicts with later mediated family traces; there is no direct family testimony | acquire direct family/community evidence or state the contradiction without resolving it |
| “The staff said…” | worker ranks, dependencies, knowledge, denials, observations, and reported speech differ | attribute each statement and preserve directness |
| “This worker was Black/white” | some historical color labels exist, but speech and occupation are not identity evidence | use only explicit, relevant, contextualized source data and specialist review |
