### GAP-011003 — Rabbinic critical-edition and dating layer

## Important but nonblocking research extensions

### GAP-011009 — Women's and non-elite voices

- Add securely provenanced bowls and amulets naming women, children, and enslaved people.
- Look for documentary papyri, letters, petitions, contracts, and geniza-adjacent late
  antique material that can move beyond normative elite texts.
- Do not use private-market objects to fill a voice gap without provenance review.

### GAP-011010 — Care implementation

- Seek archaeological and documentary evidence for lodging, food distribution, sick
  visiting, household care, and communal funds rather than using Mishnah/Bavli norms as
  administrative fact.
- Seek counterevidence: exclusion, refusal, coercive care, poverty, and stigma.

### GAP-011011 — Comparative demonology without diagnosis

- Compare 11Q11, Jewish Babylonian bowls, Josephus, and neighboring Christian, Mandaean,
  Syriac, and Mesopotamian ritual corpora through specialists.
- Track shared vocabulary, object form, and route separately. Similar beings or formulas
  do not prove the same community, meaning, or one-way borrowing.

### GAP-011012 — Dream practice and material culture

- Add amulets, dream books, inscriptions, or documentary accounts with secure provenance
  before moving from Bavli literary repertoire to daily practice.
