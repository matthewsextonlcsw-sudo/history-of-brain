## Acquisition sequence and reopening condition

Reopen substantive research only after all of the following occur:

1. A human authorizes repository contact, registration, fees and controlled access.
2. Each repository confirms holder, collection, series, call number, completeness, restrictions,
   redaction, copying, research-note, quotation, reproduction and commercial-publication terms.
3. Every acquired object is read cover to cover/page by page in supplied order; covers,
   enclosures, blanks, stamps, marginalia, missing/duplicate leaves and version relations are
   logged.
4. Restricted and sensitive files remain outside the repository and public cache. Only safe
   metadata, minimal verification excerpts and checksums enter tracked records.
5. Portuguese and any additional language/script receive qualified transcription/translation;
   editions and materially different witnesses are collated.
6. The event spine is rebuilt from contemporaneous records without overwriting the later-memory
   SRC/EVD records.
7. At least one preregistered multi-creator, multi-movement provenance sample passes object-level
   audit; a single appealing case cannot stand for the collection.
8. Creator, worker, administrator and current-institution voices remain separately attributed;
   reported speech never becomes direct testimony.
9. Brazilian archival/curatorial/legal, creator/heir, municipal/MII/SAMII/IPHAN, privacy,
   disability/survivor, labor-history, Portuguese and rights reviewers assess the rebuilt graph.
10. An independent researcher replays the pivotal images/pages, locators, source dependence,
    legal-period boundaries, privacy decisions and cache controls.
11. Only an independent closure review may change `STOP`; packet authors do not approve their own
    work.
