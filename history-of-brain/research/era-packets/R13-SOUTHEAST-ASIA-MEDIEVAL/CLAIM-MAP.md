## Disagreement records

### `DIS-022000` — what does `manoroga` establish?

- `exact_question`: Does K.209/K.368 stanza XIII document patient mental illness or a mental-health service?
- `bounded_scope`: K.209 A25–26; K.368 damaged parallel; Finot French and Honda English layers.
- `credible_positions`: Honda renders the king's `manoroga` as “disease of mind” caused by subjects'
  bodily disease; Finot renders the rhetoric as the king suffering subjects' diseases/public pain;
  downstream mental-health-service readings expand beyond both.
- `evidence_and_sources`: `EVD-022001`, `EVD-022018`, `EVD-022019`; `SRC-022000`–`SRC-022002`.
- `reason_for_difference`: witness damage, restoration, literal versus rhetorical translation, and modern category projection.
- `agreement`: royal eulogy, not a patient diagnosis, ward, or psychological treatment record.
- `expert_distribution`: unknown from this bounded replay.
- `rejected_fringe`: equivalence with psychiatry, psychotherapy, DSM/ICD categories, or a public mental-health service.
- `approved_public_wording`: none; specialist Sanskrit review pending.
- `reviewer`; `reviewed_on`: independent reviewer pending; 2026-08-18.

### `DIS-022001` — prescription, access, and poor relief

- `exact_question`: Do the four-`varṇa`, provision, and protection clauses establish universal/free care or poor relief in practice?
- `bounded_scope`: K.209 B11–D12; K.368 stanzas 19–45; Honda printed p. 409; Tonle Snguot pp. 16–17.
- `credible_positions`: the formula prescribes categories, staff, provisions, and protections; Honda infers poor relief; the Tonle report repeats broad access while saying payment is unknown.
- `evidence_and_sources`: `EVD-022002`–`EVD-022005`, `EVD-022020`–`EVD-022022`, `EVD-022008`; `SRC-022000`–`SRC-022003`.
- `reason_for_difference`: norm, social-function inference, and implementation are different layers.
- `agreement`: no opened patient or financial record establishes fees, equality, poverty status, compliance, or outcomes.
- `expert_distribution`: unknown from this bounded replay.
- `rejected_fringe`: “free universal healthcare,” modern equality, or “poorhouse” as fact.
- `approved_public_wording`: blocked pending epigraphic and social-history review.
- `reviewer`; `reviewed_on`: independent reviewer pending; 2026-08-18.

### `DIS-022002` — exchange, copying, and local agency

- `exact_question`: Is *Dharma Pātañjala* merely an Indian text copied into Java?
- `bounded_scope`: Schoemann I 21 and the opened Acri/Hunter edition-context chain.
- `credible_positions`: simple Indianization can imply one-way copying; opened philology instead describes Sanskrit materials with Old Javanese exegesis, rearrangement, addition, and Śaiva reconfiguration while acknowledging transregional Pātañjala sources.
- `evidence_and_sources`: `EVD-022010`–`EVD-022012`, `EVD-022017`, `EVD-022023`; `SRC-022004`–`SRC-022007`.
- `reason_for_difference`: diffusion model versus text-specific translation/commentary analysis.
- `agreement`: Indic-derived materials are present; exact transmission route and composition date remain uncertain.
- `expert_distribution`: unknown; principal evidence is one scholar/edition chain.
- `rejected_fringe`: pure foreign copy, pure indigenous isolation, or direct Angkor–Java borrowing.
- `approved_public_wording`: none until independent Old Javanese/Sanskrit review.
- `reviewer`; `reviewed_on`: independent reviewer pending; 2026-08-18.

### `DIS-022003` — yoga, therapy, and continuity

- `exact_question`: Can DhP mind/yoga passages be called medieval therapy, mindfulness, or a root of modern wellness?
- `bounded_scope`: DhP 194.11–13, 196.8–14, 290.10–13, 294.14–17, 298.2–4 through Acri 2022.
- `credible_positions`: modern wellness vocabulary may assimilate mind, stillness, `samādhi`, or pleasure; the text-specific analysis frames them through release, Soul, Śiva, and soteriology.
- `evidence_and_sources`: `EVD-022011`, `EVD-022013`, `EVD-022025`–`EVD-022028`; `SRC-022000`, `SRC-022003`, `SRC-022004`, `SRC-022005`.

- `agreement`: no opened source establishes a clinic, patient encounter, efficacy, prevalence, or uninterrupted transmission to modern therapy.
- `expert_distribution`: unknown.
- `rejected_fringe`: equivalence with CBT, psychotherapy, or mindfulness-based treatment.
- `approved_public_wording`: blocked.
