## Editorial and assembly dependence

Future extraction must distinguish at least these layers:

1. an event or conversation;
2. any contemporaneous notes;
3. a minute or transcript;
4. a periodical report or edited selection;
5. printing, correction, layout and image production;
6. institutional review, permission, censorship or omission;
7. later photocopy/digitization;
8. archival arrangement and description;
9. later scholarly quotation or summary.

An assembly participant is not automatically an author. A named editor or patient contributor is not proof that every page was participant-controlled. A printed “minute” is not automatically verbatim, complete, contemporaneous or consented to. A later translation is not the original voice.

## Worker and subordinate-role audit

The finding aids mention institutional correspondence, redazione, a possibly nurse-authored protest leaflet, staff meetings and assembly records. These are **role leads**, not opened worker testimony. Future replay must record:

- job/role as the source states it, without assuming professional status;
- named or anonymous authorship, hand, dictation, co-signature and representation;
- whether the worker spoke, was summarized, was discussed, or was absent;
- employment hierarchy, discipline, union/professional context and retaliation risk;
- whether editorial control belonged to a worker, patient, clinician, administrator or mixed group;
- disagreement and minority positions rather than institutional consensus.

## Gender, disability, status and coercion

- Do not infer gender participation from invitations, bylines, issue totals or one meeting description.
- Do not convert historical institutional labels into modern diagnoses, identities or capacity judgments.
- Publication, meeting attendance, chairing, signing or being quoted does not establish voluntariness or informed consent.
- Absence from a printed record may reflect access, ward segregation, literacy, language, selection, illness, disability, editorial practice or non-survival; the cause is Unknown.
- Do not create participant rosters, diagnosis cards, SEO profiles, family trees or searchable health histories.
- Any allegation, conflict, coercion, restraint, discharge hope, self-harm or violence requires a separate safety/trauma gate and exact primary locator before internal synthesis, and is not authorized for this acquisition dossier.

## Language and translation gate

The finding aids are Italian and the Setaro chapter is English, but that does not establish the frozen objects' full language state. After acquisition, log per page or folio:

- language, dialect, register, spelling, handwriting/typing and legibility;
- original quotation and diplomatic transcription before normalized transcription;
- named translator, direction and date for every translation;
- institutional, colloquial, political, emotional and diagnostic terminology in original form;
- editorial corrections and variants among physical, digital and printed witnesses;
- words supplied by brackets, ellipses, paraphrase or silent modernization;
- whether speech was contemporaneously written, recalled, summarized or translated.

Qualified Italian historical-language review is mandatory. Slovene, Friulian, German or other language expertise becomes mandatory only if an acquired object actually contains those layers; border geography alone is not proof that it does.

## Privacy, dignity and community gates

- SAN says some Basaglia/Ongaro fascicles are closed for personal-data privacy.
- The Pirella public finding aid excludes patient letters/documents, evaluations and private notebooks with sensitive data from consultation.
- ASUGI's current clinical-document route is restricted to interested/authorized people; it is not the R36 historical-archive policy, but it confirms that health documentation is not presumed open.
- Copyright, archive permission, privacy, dignity, survivor/disability review, worker safety and family/descendant interests must pass independently.
- Public or scholarly prior use does not automatically authorize Couch Commons reuse.
- Item age does not eliminate the need to minimize names and avoid sensational diagnosis, conflict, coercion or family detail.

## Missing voices that must remain visible

- people who did not or could not contribute to the periodical;
- people in closed or segregated wards;
- non-literate, disabled, non-Italian-speaking or otherwise mediated participants;
- nurses, attendants, printers, cleaners, clerks, social workers, volunteers and other subordinate workers;
- dissenters whose contributions were rejected, corrected, anonymized or never printed;
- families and support networks;
- people who refused meetings, publication, treatment or institutional representation;
- people whose records were withheld, lost, destroyed, never created or not yet catalogued.

No absence above authorizes reconstruction of inner experience. If full acquisition still cannot identify affected-person or worker voices, R36 must state that evidence ceiling and remain non-narrative.
