## Findings summary

1. The only directly established periodical holdings in the Basaglia/Ongaro finding aid are three named issues inside a mixed fascicle; this is not a complete run.
2. The complete Setaro chapter reports issue 41 plus a December 1962 special, and the Atlante card reports 42 monthly units and 41 digital fascicles. These same-contributor reports, no. 2's status, and the three-repository range map still do not resolve into one original-object and current-custodian-confirmed inventory.
3. The Basaglia/Ongaro fascicle-4 heading ends in December 1967 while its detailed list extends assembly minutes through September 1968. Both must remain visible.
4. The Pirella public inventory supports holder/provenance/privacy gates but does not independently confirm the reported issue range.
5. No frozen historical object was obtained. The packet therefore contains acquisition facts and explicit Unknowns only.
