# R36 Italy — Gorizia *Il Picchio* and Assembly Records, 1962–1968

**Status:** Reproducible acquisition dossier; substantive research `STOP`

**Place and dates:** Ospedale Psichiatrico Provinciale di Gorizia only; Aug. 1, 1962–Sept. 13,
1968. Trieste is custodial provenance or a later handoff, not part of the historical corpus.

**Question:** How did hospitalized people and staff use a periodical and recorded meetings to
negotiate daily life, work, ward authority, and public representation—and how did editorial and
institutional control shape what survives?

edited publication comparison after exact pages and rights are opened.

## Stop conditions

Catalogues and later source guides do not establish issue completeness, speaker identity,
assembly frequency, transcription authority, equal participation, or consensus. *Il Picchio*
is affected-person publication evidence only after authorship/editorial/circulation replay; it
is not unmediated or representative voice. Family-authored voice is absent. Require written
current access confirmation, call numbers, page counts, hashes, an issue/meeting provenance
matrix, Italian historical-language review plus any Slovene/German/Friulian expertise, and
health/family-data, copyright, image, naming, dignity, survivor/disability, local, and human
editorial review. No Basaglia-liberation story, Law 180 causation, work/consent/outcome claim,
patient image, name, translation, extended quotation, clinical record, or public prose.
