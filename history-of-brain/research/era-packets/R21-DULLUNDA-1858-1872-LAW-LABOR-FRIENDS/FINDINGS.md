## Findings that may carry into internal outlining

- Act XXXVI of 1858 designed several distinct custody, certification, court, inspection, friend/relative, and maintenance routes. Nothing in the packet establishes how consistently Dullunda implemented them.
- The official reports document an institutional economy in which work, workshops, outside labor, maintenance receipts, sales, estimated value, staff costs, and net cost were linked administratively.
- The labor record establishes administrative arrangements and valuation, not consent, refusal, wages to confined workers, injury, coercion, or therapeutic effect.
- Relatives and friends appear as legal applicants, custodians, maintainers, payers, and speculative motives. They do not speak in the inspected corpus.
- Visitor minutes and named case traces show official decision-making power, not first-person patient testimony.
- The complete 1867 parent item contains two administrator-mediated Cuttack speech traces. They correct the item-wide silence boundary but remain outside substantive Dullunda use and are not direct quotations.
- The 1872 report documents deficient women’s spaces through an official observer; it cannot supply women’s subjective experience.
