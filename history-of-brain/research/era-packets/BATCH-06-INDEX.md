## Why these packets belong together

Batch 06 begins the confinement, empire, and professional-authority wave without treating it as
one universal “rise of psychiatry.” Each packet owns one archive, institution, dossier, or
microcorpus. Together they compare direct self-publication, dictated and translated writing,
institution-supported print, colonial law and administrative reports, worker traces, clinical
files, family mediation, and professional authority across settings that must remain distinct.

The batch does not assume that an asylum, law, annual report, diagnosis, occupation program,
journal, patient publication, or inquiry had the same meaning or effect in St. Thomas, Utica,
Dullunda, Kingston, Mexico City, and Dumfries. Cross-packet comparison happens only after each
local evidence chain passes review.

## Search, full-source, and cache rules

- Agent Reach v1.5.0 was current at batch start. Jina Reader was available; Exa was unavailable
  because its MCP backend was not configured. Record this and use direct primary, archival,
  institutional, and specialist full-source fallback.
- Open the complete relevant source. Search snippets, OCR hits, catalogs, abstracts, and isolated
  page derivatives are leads or narrowly rated metadata unless bound to the opened parent object.
- Replay pivotal manuscript, facsimile, printed-page, interview, case-file, and tabular claims
  against the image or complete source, not OCR alone.
- Trace firstness, diagnosis, cure, reform, influence, voluntariness, coercion, treatment,
  representativeness, and institutional-change claims upstream.
- Law and policy describe authority or design; they do not prove implementation.
- Cache only lawful public-domain or openly licensed research artifacts in the ignored
  `research/source-cache/<packet-id>/` path. Record source binding, access date, exact URL,
  transformation, rights, bytes, checksum, and allowed use. Restricted, private, unclear-rights,
  or sensitive records remain link-only.

## Voice, privacy, colonial power, and harm rules

- Separate self-writing, dictated speech, translated text, petition, examination, interview,
  case note, report, institution-supported publication, private file, hearsay, and later
  scholarship. “Patient voice” is not one source type.
- Preserve the difference between an affected person's claim, an official's description, a
  family trace, a worker trace, a legal classification, and a modern historian's interpretation.
- Do not infer consent, voluntariness, coercion, efficacy, motive, recovery, or prevalence from
  silence, institutional labels, labor records, or discharge tables.
- Colonial and racialized administrative categories are historical acts of power, not neutral
  identities or present diagnoses. Do not infer race, caste, ethnicity, Nation, language, or
  community affiliation beyond the evidence.
- Minimize identifiable clinical and family detail. No raw patient dataset, searchable roster,
  case image, diagnosis card, intimate allegation, self-harm detail, or sensational SEO hook.
- Copyright, repository access, legal permission, privacy, dignity, cultural/descendant authority,
  commercial reuse, quotation, image use, and public indexing are separate gates.
- Public wording requires packet-specific language, historical, disability/survivor,
  community/descendant, privacy, rights, and harm review where named in the brief.

## Exclusions

No packet may expand into a national, imperial, Atlantic, Caribbean, African, Asian, European,
Latin American, or global asylum history. No retrospective diagnosis, treatment advice,
prevalence, population efficacy, simple humane-versus-brutal binary, or automatic progress story.
No public prose, quotation clearance, SEO/GEO content, images, reels, implementation, deployment,
or publication.

The R18 Nahua/contact-zone proposal remains `AUTHORITY_UNRESOLVED`; Batch 06 does not activate or
circumvent it.

## Cross-review gate

Packet authors cannot approve their own work. Every packet receives an independent risk-based
review, mandatory repair pass, and independent closure replay before coordinator synthesis or
internal outline extraction. `PASS WITH GAPS` never removes language, archive, privacy, harm,
rights, community, descendant, disability/survivor, or human editorial publication holds.
