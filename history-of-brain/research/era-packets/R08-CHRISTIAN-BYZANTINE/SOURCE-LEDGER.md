## Method, boundary, and search record

- Ratings follow the project model: L1 direct/authoritative evidence; L2 specialist scholarship; L3 authoritative reference or accountable translation; L4 accountable secondary synthesis; L5 discovery lead; LX rejected or unusable. Claim fit is A direct, B strong indirect, C context, D mismatch.
- Agent Reach health checking found Jina Reader available and Exa unavailable. Full reachable pages and PDFs were opened with Jina or directly; built-in primary-source web search supplied the discovery fallback. Search snippets, abstracts, handouts, publisher descriptions, and tables of contents were leads only.
- The executable packet was narrowed from the 200–800 CE brief to a 300–600 CE core: Greek-speaking eastern Roman contexts (especially Egypt, Cappadocia, and Constantinople), with Augustine as one explicitly marked Latin North African comparator. Greek and Latin texts were accessed through named or edition-identifiable English translations; the researcher does not claim independent Greek, Latin, or Coptic competence.
- Excluded from factual synthesis for this pass: 200–300 and 600–800 CE; Syriac, Armenian, Georgian, Ethiopic, and most Coptic evidence; western Latin Europe beyond Augustine; detailed Jewish, pagan, and medical continuities; archaeological excavation evidence; and Islamicate hospital/translation networks. These are handoffs, not evidence of absence.
- Ideals, literary representation, rules, law, institutional operation, and lived or mediated voice are separate evidence classes. A legal rule does not prove implementation; a hagiographic cure does not prove diagnosis or efficacy; a monastic prescription does not prove compliance.
- `opened_by`: R08 packet researcher for every entry. `opened_on`: 2026-08-18.

## Rejected, quarantined, and missing evidence

- Search-result snippets and popular retellings calling Basilias the “first hospital,” “first psychiatric hospital,” or proof of a Christian invention were not evidence. The checked upstream dispute is `SRC-012005` plus the methodological caution in `SRC-012006`.
- Pirated copies and reposted books were rejected. No protected full text was placed in the packet or cache.
- No archaeological excavation report sufficiently specific to institutional care in the narrowed period was opened. The packet therefore makes no archaeological claim.
- No direct patient register, poor recipient's letter, institutional worker account, or named enslaved person's care testimony was located in this pass.
- The lack of a usable full text for Basil Q55 prevents a responsible positive synthesis about learned medicine and ascetic medicine from that source.
