# R13-SOUTH-ASIA-MEDIEVAL — gaps and handoff

**State:** `PASS WITH GAPS` after independent closure; internal reconciliation only; no public prose approved
**Packet date:** 2026-08-18
**Coverage:** three bounded settings only: *Mādhavanidāna* chapter 20; the Tirumukkūḍal inscription of Vīrarājendra's sixth regnal year (late eleventh century); and received *Tiruvācakam* poem 4 plus selected transmission evidence

## Executive disposition

R13 supports an internal outline about three different kinds of evidence: a Sanskrit diagnostic taxonomy, a Tamil administrative record of endowed care, and a Tamil devotional poem's representation of embodied intensity and social mockery. It does **not** support a unified “medieval South Asian mind,” a survey of South Asia, a history of psychiatric institutions, a patient-experience narrative, or any priority/continuity slogan.

The strongest anchors are the exact printed Sanskrit loci in *Mādhavanidāna* 20.1–34; the Tirumukkūḍal inscription's recorded beneficiary, staffing, ration, and medicine allocations; the Tamil wording and reception of poem 4; and explicit archive limits. The sharpest negative finding is that the inscription does not state medicine indications or identify mental/behavioral conditions. A modern psychiatric article acknowledges that absence and then infers mental treatment from other medical texts. The positive mental-treatment claim therefore remains quarantined.

## Mandatory gaps

### `GAP-019500` — Sanskrit critical edition and translation

- **Problem:** `SRC-019000` is an opened 1943 printed Sanskrit witness with later commentary, not a modern critical edition or manuscript collation. The packet preserves exact Sanskrit strings but supplies no publication-ready independent translation.
- **Repair:** A Sanskrit/Ayurvedic textual specialist must replay *Mādhavanidāna* 20.1–34 against a current critical edition and manuscript apparatus, identify commentary layers and variants, and provide passage-specific translations of *unmāda*, *mānasa vyādhi*, *mānasa duḥkha*, *doṣa*, and *graha* language.
- **Gate:** `CLM-019201–019203` remain internal paraphrase/architecture claims; no public quotation or modern diagnostic English.

### `GAP-019501` — composition date and place

- **Problem:** The approximate c. 700 date is a modern editorial proposal; the selected evidence does not establish an exact date, author biography, or composition place.
- **Repair:** Open the upstream dating scholarship and critical-edition introductions; record proposed termini, manuscript evidence, quotation history, attribution problems, and rival chronologies.
- **Gate:** `CLM-019204` may say “modern editors place/propose,” never exact year, birthplace, court, or region of composition.

### `GAP-019502` — Arabic reception and translation chain

- **Problem:** `SRC-019001` identifies the Arabic *Nidān* and reports probable translation/reception, but direct Arabic witnesses, translators, dates, passages, and surviving manuscripts were not independently reconstructed.
- **Repair:** Trace the named Arabic work and quotations through critical Arabic editions, manuscript records, and specialist transmission scholarship. Separate translation, title equivalence, quotation, catalog reference, and later report.
- **Gate:** No simple “India gave psychiatry to the Islamic world,” one-way diffusion, or continuous-practice claim.

### `GAP-019503` — Tamil epigraphic replay

- **Problem:** Ayyar's edition and translation are the primary conduit. Role names, damaged readings, the regnal-year/date conversion, beneficiary language, and ll. 42–45 have not received a second Tamil epigraphist check.
- **Repair:** Commission an epigraphist to replay the estampage/stone where accessible, Tamil and Grantha forms, damaged loci, date calculation, personnel terms, rations, remuneration, medicines, and beneficiary language. Explicitly test later claims about worker gender rather than importing them from modern paraphrase.
- **Gate:** `CLM-019205–019206` remain “the inscription records/provides for”; gendered staffing and universal/free access wording are blocked.

### `GAP-019504` — operation, access, costs, and outcomes at Tirumukkūḍal

- **Problem:** An endowment record is not an observation of implementation. The packet found no admission criteria, surrounding-population access, actual occupancy, treatment encounter, cost to recipients, refusal, coercion, recovery, adverse outcome, or institutional duration.
- **Repair:** Search connected inscriptions, accounting records, archaeology, local histories, and comparative Chola administrative records; distinguish allocation, capacity, observed delivery, and retrospective reconstruction.
- **Gate:** No “fully functioning,” “public,” “free for all,” “successful,” or patient-outcome prose.

### `GAP-019505` — Tirumukkūḍal mental-treatment claim

- **Repair:** A positive claim would require a site-linked indication, case, prescription, or equivalent direct record plus philological review. If no such evidence appears, publish only the evidence-limit explanation.
