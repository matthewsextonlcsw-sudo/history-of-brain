# R13-SOUTH-ASIA-MEDIEVAL — Medicine, Devotion, Ethics, Law, and Household Worlds

**State:** Authorized; research only

**Identifier range:** `019000–019999`

## Research question

What can a small set of named South Asian texts, inscriptions, institutions, biographies,
household records, and material contexts from approximately 600 to 1500 establish about mind,
emotion, suffering, altered conduct, care, cultivation, devotion, medicine, law, and everyday
coping after the classical corpora?

## Boundary

Choose no more than three named regional/corpus settings and a small primary corpus. Sanskrit,
Pali, Prakrit, Tamil, Persian, Arabic, Tibetan, or vernacular evidence may enter only through
named local settings and accountable editions. Separate court, temple/monastic, medical,
devotional, legal, and household archives. Do not claim subcontinental representation.

## Required evidence

- critical or authoritative source texts/translations, inscriptions, manuscripts, archival or
  object records with exact locators;
- specialist regional, linguistic, medical, religious, philosophical, legal, gender, caste,
  disability, and social history;
- exact treatment of selected terms for mind, emotion, suffering, conduct, illness, devotion,
  and care without modern-diagnosis equivalence;
- textual norm versus implementation, and elite prescription versus household/non-elite life;
- interactions among medicine, devotion, ritual, ethics, law, family, and political power
  without a religion-versus-science ladder;
- women, caste/status, labor, poverty, disability, dependence, cost, coercion, consent, refusal,
  and outcome where evidence permits;
- transmission/translation/contact claims linked to named texts, people, or routes; and
- audits of timeless Ayurveda/yoga/mindfulness, first psychotherapy, universal tolerance, and
  unbroken wellness-continuity claims.

## Stop

Do not cover all medieval South Asia, repeat the Batch 02 classical corpus as later continuity,
or use colonial/modern summaries as direct medieval evidence. Create five standard outputs and
rights-safe ignored cache; no public prose.
