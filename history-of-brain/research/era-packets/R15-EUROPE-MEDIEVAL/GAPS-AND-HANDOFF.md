## Cross-packet reconciliation warnings

1. **Do not let Latin Christendom stand for Europe.** These are three settings, mostly elite and Christian.
2. **Do not erase Islamicate authorship.** Constantine's text sits in an Arabic–Latin chain; attribution and adaptation must remain visible.
3. **Do not compare institutions by name alone.** A priory, hospital, shrine, household, court custody, and later asylum are not automatically commensurable.
4. **Do not compare frequencies from unlike archives.** Miracle collections select cures; law selects property; medical books select learned doctrine.
5. **Do not make teleology from terms.** `Melancholia`, `phrenesis`, `amentia`, `non compos mentis`, and possession language do not form a straight path to modern psychiatry.
6. **Do not treat compassion or cruelty as a single epoch trait.** Record specific support, restraint, cost, custody, coercion, and claimed benefit separately.
