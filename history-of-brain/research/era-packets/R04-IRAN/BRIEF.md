# R04-IRAN — Body, Breath, Soul, Ethics, Healing, and Empire

**State:** Authorized; research only

**Identifier range:** `007000–007999`

## Research question

How do securely dated and located Iranian-language, Zoroastrian, imperial, medical, legal,
material, and later transmitted sources represent body, breath, soul, emotion, suffering,
conduct, healing, pollution, care, and social response—and what chronology and transmission
limits prevent a timeless “Persian view of mind”?

## Boundary

Named Iranian polities, languages, communities, and corpora from approximately 1200 BCE to
700 CE. Separate composition, redaction, manuscript, and translation dates. The packet must
select a manageable core corpus and may not use later Pahlavi manuscripts as transparent
Bronze/Iron Age witnesses.

## Required evidence

- critical editions/translations with exact passage and version;
- specialist Iranian/Zoroastrian philology and history;
- material, legal, household, healing, and institutional evidence where available;
- explicit dating/transmission disputes;
- non-Zoroastrian and internal variation rather than imperial or religious uniformity;
- reception-chain audits for “first hospital,” “first psychology,” dualism, demonology, and
  modern-diagnosis claims; and
- archive, colonial collection, language, and missing-voice analysis.

## Stop

Do not expand into the medieval Persianate/Islamicate packet or use later medical synthesis to
fill earlier gaps. Create the five standard outputs and local ignored cache; no public prose.
