## Handoff seams

### Earlier and neighboring histories

- **Mesopotamia/Levant:** South-Iraqi bowls belong to a Mesopotamian ritual-text corpus as much as a
  Sasanian imperial context. Coordinate rather than claim them as “Persian medicine.”
- **South Asia:** Avestan/Old Indic linguistic and ritual comparisons require demonstrable textual
  method; similarity is not automatic borrowing. Later Indian Zoroastrian manuscript custody is a
  transmission history, not evidence for ancient India.
- **Egypt/Greek/Roman worlds:** Greek physicians at Persian courts and later Greek/Syriac medical
  transmission are leads, but “Persia imported Greek rational medicine” is too simple. Trace people,
  dates, texts, and languages in exchange packets.
- **Central Asia:** eastern Iranian geography, Sogdian/Bactrian traditions, Buddhism, Manichaeism,
  and Silk Road manuscript cultures need dedicated packet coordination.

### Islamicate and late-antique continuation

- Hand off Gondēšāpur's 765 hospital mention and Boḵtīšūʿ family to the Islamicate institutions and
  translation-network packet.
- Hand off ninth-century *Dēnkard*, *Bundahišn*, and Zādspram medical/body-soul material as early-
  Islamic-period Middle Persian texts that may preserve/rework older traditions; do not backdate
  automatically.
- Continue Syriac, Greek, Middle Persian, Sanskrit, and Arabic exchange through named translators,
  courts, schools, monasteries, texts, and surviving manuscripts—not civilizational relay language.
- Treat the Arab conquest as a political transition, not an instant end of Iranian, Zoroastrian,
  Christian, Jewish, or local medical practice.

### Modern reception and SEO/GEO claims

- Route “Zoroaster invented free will,” “Persians invented psychiatry,” “Gondēšāpur was the world's
  first hospital/university,” “Kartīr massacred every minority,” and “Persian medicine combined magic
  and science” through `CLM-007015–CLM-007020` and the shared-chain audit before any public answer
  box. The first-psychology, invented-psychiatry, and magic/science formulations remain unverified
  discovery questions, not corrections ready to publish.
- Search rankings are evidence of modern reception only. They do not increase ancient-source
  confidence.
- For concise public answers later, pair the familiar claim with the bounded correction, exact
  locator, transmission note, and remaining uncertainty.
