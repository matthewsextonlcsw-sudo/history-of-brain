## Method and boundary

The packet retains three settings only:

1. Sun Simiao's *Beiji qianjin yaofang* and *Qianjin yifang*, composed in the Tang but available here through Song editorial and later print witnesses;
2. Xu Shuwei's securely attributed *Puji benshifang* in the Southern Song Zhenzhou print/case setting, with *Shanghan jiushi lun* retained only as a disputed later-attributed transmission problem; and
3. Yuan Cai's 1178 household manual and Song Ci's 1247 death-investigation compilation, two separate Southern Song official-text settings joined only for a bounded comparison of household hierarchy, coercion, self-harm, and state scrutiny.

Work date, commentary date, edition date, and present digital surrogate are separate fields. A prescription is not evidence of implementation. A physician's successful case narrative is not transparent observation. A household manual's scenario is not a servant's testimony. A death-investigation category is not a modern diagnosis.

Agent Reach v1.5.0 `doctor --json` reported Jina Reader available. Exa was unavailable because `mcporter` plus the Exa MCP route was not configured. Jina/full-document access, direct institutional repositories, and built-in primary/scholarly web search were used as the documented fallback. Abstracts and snippets were leads only. All rows were opened by “R13 Codex packet researcher” on 2026-08-18.
