## Batch decision

This batch tests how postwar law, expert committees, decolonizing institutions, community-care
claims, affected-person publications, families, workers, and cultural collections entered the
record. It must not become a parade of celebrated reformers. Each packet separates program or
legal design from implementation, direct experience from professional representation, and
later movement memory from contemporaneous evidence.

The feasibility screen also audited Fann/Dakar and AIIMH/Bangalore. Both remain deferred rather
than activated: the Fann lane depends on a restricted pseudonymized clinical dossier and
unopened core publications; the AIIMH lane depends on institution-held training and casework
objects whose complete texts, privacy terms, and reuse conditions are unresolved.

## Full-source and acquisition rules

- Open and replay complete relevant objects. Abstracts, catalogues, snippets, OCR hits, later
  quotations, and institutional timelines are discovery or bounded metadata unless their exact
  claim fit says otherwise.
- Cache only lawful public-domain or openly licensed research artifacts in ignored
  `research/source-cache/<packet-id>/` directories. Record source binding, exact URL, access
  date, transformation, bytes, page or object count, checksum, rights, and allowed use.
- Restricted, private, culturally governed, unclear-rights, sensitive clinical, artwork, film,
  and archival objects remain link-only unless written authority permits a safer handling plan.
- An acquisition `STOP` must still be reproducible: identify the exact object, holder, call
  number or issue, access route, missing components, rights/authority decision, and the precise
  condition that would reopen research.

## Voice, language, power, and dignity rules

- A law, committee report, program article, annual report, museum timeline, or clinician's
  retrospective is direct for its author's position—not for delivery, consent, effectiveness,
  patient experience, or national transformation.
- Family/protector consent is not patient consent. Negotiation with elders, institutions, or
  custodians is not consent from every affected person, relative, host, worker, or creator.
- A patient periodical, assembly minute, interview, artwork collection, or translated clinical
  statement is mediated. Record author/speaker, editor, recorder, interpreter, translator,
  selection, intended audience, and institutional control.
- Preserve Japanese, Yoruba and other Nigerian languages, French, Arabic, Kabyle/Tamazight,
  Portuguese, Italian, and any Slovene/German/Friulian layers item by item. Do not infer a spoken
  language from administrative language or identity labels.
- No retrospective diagnosis, efficacy, prevalence, consent, recovery, voluntariness, or
  firstness claim. No patient face, clinical image, artwork, signature, family graph, named
  medical case, long quotation, or searchable roster.

## Packet-specific ceilings

- **Japan:** government “community care” is a program description; prefectural/Kanagawa data
  cannot become a national lived-experience sample. Direct patient voice remains missing.
- **WHO:** committee recommendations are collective expert views, not binding WHO policy or
  country implementation. The membership/voice-absence and multilingual edition layers are
  central evidence.
- **Aro:** organizers document selection, boarding, relatives, treatment, and village structure;
  they do not supply direct patient, family, host, nurse, healer, labor, consent, cost, or outcome
  testimony.
- **Blida:** no substantive claim until the declared *Notre Journal* issue-units and companion
  primary objects are completely and lawfully opened.
- **Brazil MII:** current custody, heritage status, physical possession, copyright, creator
  agency, medical privacy, and permission to narrate or reproduce are separate. No artwork use.
- **Gorizia:** no substantive voice claim until the surviving *Il Picchio* run and assembly
  records are acquired, ordered, and audited. Family-authored voice is absent.
