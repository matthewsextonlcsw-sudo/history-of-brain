# P03 — Greece

> *Greece is where the mind stopped being a ghost and started being a body problem. Hippocrates wrote it down. The Hippocratic Corpus is in libraries today. You can read what he wrote. That is the point.*

## Draft

Hippocrates wrote that mental illness comes from the body, not from the gods. That was a shift. Before him, a person who was losing their mind was possessed. After him, the same person had an imbalance of humors. The body was the problem. The body was the solution.

The Hippocratic Corpus is about 40 texts. Some are surgical. Some are dietary. Some are about the mind. The text "On the Sacred Disease" is the one that matters here. It says epilepsy is not a divine punishment. It is a body problem. The brain is involved. That is a specific claim, made in the 5th century BCE, written down in a text you can read today.

The Wellcome Collection has a translation. The NLM has a digitized edition. The British Museum has fragments. You can check all three.

The shift from "possessed" to "imbalance" is not a jump from superstition to science. It is a shift in where the cause lives. The gods are out. The body is in. That is the Greek contribution to the history of the mind.
