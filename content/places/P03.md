## Draft

This is the world of the brain, soul, regimen, and sanctuary. [Hippocrates' On the Sacred Disease](https://classics.mit.edu/Hippocrates/sacred.html) reframes epilepsy as a natural brain disorder — a move that sounds modern, but the tradition never escapes the entanglement of mind, spirit, and community. The [backbone's B2.1 node](https://github.com/matthewsexton/history-of-brain/blob/main/history-of-brain/outline/CHRONOLOGICAL-BACKBONE.md) shows this in the earliest written records: the brain, soul, regimen, and sanctuary.

The [Lloyd & Bowen account](https://doi.org/10.1525/9780520964800) shows the heart–brain debate in Greek medicine. The brain is one answer. The sanctuary is another. The regimen is a third. They coexist. They do not replace each other.

The [backbone's B2.2 node](https://github.com/matthewsexton/history-of-brain/blob/main/history-of-brain/outline/CHRONOLOGICAL-BACKBONE.md) shows Roman imperial pluralism: household remedies, itinerant healers, temples, learned medicine, Galenic synthesis, law, status, and reported patient speech. [Celsus 3.18](https://scaife.perseus.org/reader/urn:cts:latinLit:phi0836.phi002.perseus-lat5:3.18/?right=perseus-eng2) shows the plural healing landscape. The healing landscape is not a hierarchy. It is a market — but a market with power, status, and cost built into every transaction.

The [backbone's B2.7 node](https://github.com/matthewsexton/history-of-brain/blob/main/history-of-brain/outline/CHRONOLOGICAL-BACKBONE.md) asks who had a choice of healer. The answer depends on status, gender, enslavement, household authority, cost, and pilgrimage. The "medical marketplace" is not a free market. It is a market with power built into every transaction.

The [backbone's B0 lived-experience rule](https://github.com/matthewsexton/history-of-brain/blob/main/history-of-brain/outline/CHRONOLOGICAL-BACKBONE.md) is blunt: no direct voice survives. Say so prominently. The [evidence model's voice rule](https://github.com/matthewsexton/history-of-brain/blob/main/history-of-brain/research/era-packets/BATCH-08-SEAM-SYNTHESIS.md) is blunt: protector, elder, clinician, archive, or institutional speech is not family or patient consent. Do not infer burden, support, abandonment, motive, or consensus.

The [backbone's B4.9 node](https://github.com/matthewsexton/history-of-brain/blob/main/history-of-brain/outline/CHRONOLOGICAL-BACKBONE.md) asks who gets believed in the Ann Pratt official dossier. The archive structurally underrepresents people outside the property-and-court jurisdiction. The [UK National Archives](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/help-with-your-research/research-guides/mental-health/) warns that many mental-health patient files were destroyed. The silence is not absence of resistance or consent. It is the result of a specific, traceable act of destruction.

The transition is not a clean break. The Greek-speaking Mediterranean does not end. It changes. The next band picks up where these variations leave off — not where they end, because they do not end.
