## Draft

The Enlightenment is where the mind became a scientific problem. Before the 18th century, the mind was a religious problem or a legal problem. The 18th century made it a scientific one. The mind could be studied. The mind could be measured. The mind could be treated.

The Wellcome Collection holds materials from this period. They show the shift from religious to scientific explanation. The shift from prayer to medicine. The shift from exorcism to treatment.

The NLM holds digitized editions of the key texts. You can read them. You can see what the Enlightenment doctors thought. You can see what they did not think. You can see where they disagreed with the Church.

The British Museum holds texts and documents from this period. They show the medical practice. They show the scientific practice. They show the gap between the two.

The Enlightenment contribution is not a story of progress. It is a story of a shift. The shift from religion to science. The shift from prayer to medicine. The records show the shift. The gaps show what the scientists did not want to say.
