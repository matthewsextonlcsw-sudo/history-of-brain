## Draft

The 21st century is where the mind became a digital problem. Before the 2000s, the mind was a rights problem. The 2000s made it a digital one. The mind could be tracked. The mind could be monitored. The mind could be data.

The Wellcome Collection holds materials from this period. They show the shift from rights-based to digital treatment. The shift from advocacy to data. The shift from dignity to surveillance.

The NLM holds digitized editions of the key texts. You can read them. You can see what the 21st-century researchers thought. You can see what they did not think. You can see where they disagreed with the advocates.

The National Archives holds records of the Ministry of Health from this period. The MH series holds correspondence, reports, and administrative records. You can search them online.

The 21st-century contribution is not a story of progress. It is a story of a shift. The shift from rights to data. The shift from advocacy to surveillance. The records show the shift. The gaps show what the researchers did not want to say.
