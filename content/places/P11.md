The early 20th century is where the mind became a psychological problem. Before the 20th century, the mind was a medical problem. The 20th century made it a psychological one. The mind could be studied. The mind could be measured. The mind could be treated.

The Wellcome Collection holds materials from this period. They show the shift from medical to psychological explanation. The shift from body to mind. The shift from treatment to therapy.

The NLM holds digitized editions of the key texts. You can read them. You can see what the early 20th-century psychologists thought. You can see what they did not think. You can see where they disagreed with the doctors.

The National Archives holds records of the Ministry of Health from this period. The MH series holds correspondence, reports, and administrative records. You can search them online.
