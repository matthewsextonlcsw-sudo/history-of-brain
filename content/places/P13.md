## Draft

This is the world of the MII, the anti-asylum movement, and the 1990 Caracas Declaration. The [R35 Brazil MII packet](https://github.com/matthewsexton/history-of-brain/blob/main/history-of-brain/research/era-packets/BATCH-08-SEAM-SYNTHESIS.md) is an acquisition STOP dossier — the evidence is not yet available. The [backbone's B8.5 node](https://github.com/matthewsexton/history-of-brain/blob/main/history-of-brain/outline/CHRONOLOGICAL-BACKBONE.md) shows the right to leave the hospital—and the right to real support: Brazil's anti-asylum movement, the [1990 Caracas Declaration](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789241580520), Italy's influence, Ghanaian user organizing, and the gap between closure and care.

The [backbone's B2.7 node](https://github.com/matthewsexton/history-of-brain/blob/main/history-of-brain/outline/CHRONOLOGICAL-BACKBONE.md) asks who had a choice of healer. The answer depends on status, gender, enslavement, household authority, cost, and pilgrimage. The "medical marketplace" is not a free market. It is a market with power built into every transaction.

The [backbone's B0 lived-experience rule](https://github.com/matthewsexton/history-of-brain/blob/main/history-of-brain/outline/CHRONOLOGICAL-BACKBONE.md) is blunt: no direct voice survives. Say so prominently. The [evidence model's voice rule](https://github.com/matthewsexton/history-of-brain/blob/main/history-of-brain/research/era-packets/BATCH-08-SEAM-SYNTHESIS.md) is blunt: protector, elder, clinician, archive, or institutional speech is not family or patient consent. Do not infer burden, support, abandonment, motive, or consensus.

The [backbone's B4.9 node](https://github.com/matthewsexton/history-of-brain/blob/main/history-of-brain/outline/CHRONOLOGICAL-BACKBONE.md) asks who gets believed in the Ann Pratt official dossier. The archive structurally underrepresents people outside the property-and-court jurisdiction. The [UK National Archives](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/help-with-your-research/research-guides/mental-health/) warns that many mental-health patient files were destroyed. The silence is not absence of resistance or consent. It is the result of a specific, traceable act of destruction.

The [backbone's B6.5 node](https://github.com/matthewsexton/history-of-brain/blob/main/history-of-brain/outline/CHRONOLOGICAL-BACKBONE.md) shows the drug in colonial and postcolonial contexts: the drug travels, and it changes as it travels. The [backbone's B7.1 node](https://github.com/matthewsexton/history-of-brain/blob/main/history-of-brain/outline/CHRONOLOGICAL-BACKBONE.md) shows the institution and the family: the family is the first institution. The family was the first institution of mental health, and it was also the first institution of control.

The transition is not a clean break. Brazil does not end. It changes. The next band picks up where these variations leave off — not where they end, because they do not end. The anti-asylum movement is not a single event. It is a long, contested, and still-ongoing process. The gap between closure and care is not a failure of the movement. It is the structure of the system.
