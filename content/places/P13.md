## Draft

Europe is where the asylum was born. The 19th century is the part that matters here. The Lunacy Act of 1845 in England required every county to build an asylum. The County Asylums Act of 1808 was the first step. The records are in the National Archives today. You can check them.

The Wellcome Collection has materials. The British Museum has related materials. You can check them.

The shift from the medieval period to the 19th century is not a break. It is a continuation. The mind was still a body problem. The humors were still the explanation. But the scale was different. Europe had more people, more cities, more institutions. The mind was a public problem, not just a private one.
