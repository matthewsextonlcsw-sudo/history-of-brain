## Draft

Mesoamerica has a medical tradition that is older than Europe. The role of the shaman is the part that matters here. The shaman was not a doctor. The shaman was a mediator between the living and the dead, between the human and the divine. The mind was not a body problem. The mind was a spiritual problem.

The records are in museums and archives today. You can check them.

The Wellcome Collection has materials. The NLM has digitized editions. The British Museum has related materials. You can check them.

The shift from Europe to Mesoamerica is not a break. It is a different starting point. The mind is not a body problem. The mind is a spiritual problem. That is a different way of thinking about the mind.
