The 19th century is where the mind became a medical and legal problem at the same time. The asylum was built. The law was passed. The Lunacy Commission was established. The mind was now a matter of state policy.

The National Archives guide is clear: "Lunatic asylums were first established in Britain in the mid-19th century." The 1845 Acts made county lunatic asylums compulsory. The Lunacy Commission was established to regulate them.

The Wellcome Collection holds materials from this period. They show the medical practice. They show the legal practice. They show the gap between the two.
