## Draft

This is the world of *xin* as heart-mind, emotion, conduct, and order. The [backbone's B2.4 node](https://github.com/matthewsexton/history-of-brain/blob/main/history-of-brain/outline/CHRONOLOGICAL-BACKBONE.md) shows this in the earliest written records: *xin* as heart-mind, emotion, conduct, and order.

The [SEP entry](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/chinese-mind/) shows *xin* as a single embodied locus where thought, emotion, and moral character intertwine. It is not a location. It is a whole. The promise was order. The violence was the order itself — the confinement, the public performance, the family's authority.

The [backbone's B2.7 node](https://github.com/matthewsexton/history-of-brain/blob/main/history-of-brain/outline/CHRONOLOGICAL-BACKBONE.md) asks who had a choice of healer. The answer depends on status, gender, enslavement, household authority, cost, and pilgrimage. The "medical marketplace" is not a free market. It is a market with power built into every transaction.

The [backbone's B0 lived-experience rule](https://github.com/matthewsexton/history-of-brain/blob/main/history-of-brain/outline/CHRONOLOGICAL-BACKBONE.md) is blunt: no direct voice survives. Say so prominently. The [evidence model's voice rule](https://github.com/matthewsexton/history-of-brain/blob/main/history-of-brain/research/era-packets/BATCH-08-SEAM-SYNTHESIS.md) is blunt: protector, elder, clinician, archive, or institutional speech is not family or patient consent. Do not infer burden, support, abandonment, motive, or consensus.

The [backbone's B4.9 node](https://github.com/matthewsexton/history-of-brain/blob/main/history-of-brain/outline/CHRONOLOGICAL-BACKBONE.md) asks who gets believed in the Ann Pratt official dossier. The archive structurally underrepresents people outside the property-and-court jurisdiction. The [UK National Archives](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/help-with-your-research/research-guides/mental-health/) warns that many mental-health patient files were destroyed. The silence is not absence of resistance or consent. It is the result of a specific, traceable act of destruction.

The [backbone's B4.1 node](https://github.com/matthewsexton/history-of-brain/blob/main/history-of-brain/outline/CHRONOLOGICAL-BACKBONE.md) traces Persianate and South Asian entanglements: Unani, Ayurveda, Siddha, Buddhist, court, shrine, household, and market practices. The ruling is explicit: follow the practices rather than inventing a single imported or indigenous line. The [backbone's B6.5 node](https://github.com/matthewsexton/history-of-brain/blob/main/history-of-brain/outline/CHRONOLOGICAL-BACKBONE.md) shows the drug in colonial and postcolonial contexts: the drug travels, and it changes as it travels.

The [backbone's B7.1 node](https://github.com/matthewsexton/history-of-brain/blob/main/history-of-brain/outline/CHRONOLOGICAL-BACKBONE.md) shows the institution and the family: the family is the first institution. The family was the first institution of mental health, and it was also the first institution of control.

The transition is not a clean break. China does not end. It changes. The next band picks up where these variations leave off — not where they end, because they do not end.
