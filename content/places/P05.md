# P05 — India

> *India has a medical tradition that is older than Greece. The Charaka Samhita and the Sushruta Samhita are the two texts that matter here. They describe the body, the mind, and the relationship between them. They are in libraries today. You can read them.*

## Draft

The Charaka Samhita describes the mind as part of the body. It is not a ghost. It is not a soul. It is a function of the body, like the heart or the liver. The text says the mind is affected by the same things that affect the body: diet, climate, emotion, and injury.

The Sushruta Samhita is a surgical text. It describes 300 surgical procedures. Some are for the body. Some are for the mind. The text says that mental illness can be treated with surgery, with diet, and with herbs. That is a different approach from Greece. The mind is not just a body problem. It is a whole-person problem.

The Wellcome Collection has translations. The NLM has digitized editions. The British Museum has related materials. You can check them.

The Indian contribution is not a break from Greece. It is a different starting point. The mind is part of the body, but it is also part of the whole person. That is a different way of thinking about the mind.
