## Draft

China is where the mind became a medical and philosophical problem. The Chinese medical tradition treated mental illness as a body problem. The philosophical tradition treated it as a mind problem. The two traditions ran in parallel. They did not always agree.

The traditional Chinese medicine texts describe mental illness in terms of the body. The qi (life force) is out of balance. The treatment is herbal. The treatment is acupuncture. The treatment is lifestyle. The mind is a function of the body.

The philosophical texts describe mental illness in terms of the mind. The mind is in distress. The treatment is meditation. The treatment is reflection. The treatment is discipline. The mind is a spiritual entity.

The Wellcome Collection holds materials from both traditions. The NLM holds digitized editions with English translations. You can read the texts. You can see what the medical doctors thought. You can see what the philosophers thought. You can see where they disagreed.

The British Museum holds manuscripts and documents from this period. They show the medical practice. They show the philosophical practice. They show the gap between the two.

The traditional Chinese medicine texts are the body system. The philosophical texts are the mind system. The two systems ran in parallel. They did not always agree. The medical doctor treated the body. The philosopher treated the mind. The patient received both. The record shows both. The gap shows what each system did not want to say.

The traditional Chinese medicine texts describe mental illness in terms of the body. The qi is out of balance. The treatment is herbal, acupuncture, and lifestyle. The philosophical texts describe mental illness in terms of the mind. The mind is in distress. The treatment is meditation, reflection, and discipline. The two systems ran in parallel. The patient received both.

The Chinese contribution is not a story of progress. It is a story of two systems running in parallel. The body system. The mind system. The records show both. The gaps show what each system did not want to say.
