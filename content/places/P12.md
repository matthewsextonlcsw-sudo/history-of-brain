The mid 20th century is where the mind became a pharmacological problem. Before the 1950s, the mind was a psychological problem. The 1950s made it a pharmacological one. The mind could be treated with drugs. The mind could be calmed. The mind could be controlled.

The Wellcome Collection holds materials from this period. They show the shift from psychological to pharmacological treatment. The shift from therapy to medication. The shift from talking to pills.

The NLM holds digitized editions of the key texts. You can read them. You can see what the mid 20th-century psychiatrists thought. You can see what they did not think. You can see where they disagreed with the psychologists.

The National Archives holds records of the Ministry of Health from this period. The MH series holds correspondence, reports, and administrative records. You can search them online.
