## Draft

The present day is where the mind is a digital, pharmacological, and rights problem at the same time. The mind is tracked. The mind is treated. The mind is advocated. The three systems run in parallel. They do not always agree.

The Wellcome Collection holds materials from this period. They show the three systems. They show the gaps between them. They show what each system does not want to say.

The NLM holds digitized editions of the key texts. You can read them. You can see what the present-day researchers thought. You can see what they did not think. You can see where they disagreed with each other.

The National Archives holds records of the Ministry of Health from this period. The MH series holds correspondence, reports, and administrative records. You can search them online.

The present-day contribution is not a story of progress. It is a story of three systems running in parallel. The digital system. The pharmacological system. The rights system. The records show all three. The gaps show what each system did not want to say.
