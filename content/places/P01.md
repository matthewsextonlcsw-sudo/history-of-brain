## Draft

This is the first written record of distress. The [Electronic Babylonian Library](https://www.ebl.lmu.de/) records diagnostic tablets that locate thought, emotion, and decision-making in the heart. The [backbone's B1.1 node](https://github.com/matthewsexton/history-of-brain/blob/main/history-of-brain/outline/CHRONOLOGICAL-BACKBONE.md) shows this in the earliest written records: heart-centered explanations, ritual, and the first medical texts.

The practice was ritual and exorcism. The promise was restoration. The violence was the ritual itself — the binding, the confinement, the public performance. The [backbone's B1.2 node](https://github.com/matthewsexton/history-of-brain/blob/main/history-of-brain/outline/CHRONOLOGICAL-BACKBONE.md) shows the same pattern in Egypt: family petitions and heart-centered vocabularies. The family was the first institution of mental health, and it was also the first institution of control.

The [backbone's B2.1 node](https://github.com/matthewsexton/history-of-brain/blob/main/history-of-brain/outline/CHRONOLOGICAL-BACKBONE.md) shows the Greek-speaking Mediterranean: the brain, soul, regimen, and sanctuary. The [Lloyd & Bowen account](https://doi.org/10.1525/9780520964800) shows the heart–brain debate in Greek medicine. The practice was medical and ritual. The promise was cure. The violence was the regimen — the diet, the exercise, the confinement.

The [backbone's B2.7 node](https://github.com/matthewsexton/history-of-brain/blob/main/history-of-brain/outline/CHRONOLOGICAL-BACKBONE.md) asks who had a choice of healer. The answer depends on status, gender, enslavement, household authority, cost, and pilgrimage. The "medical marketplace" is not a free market. It is a market with power built into every transaction.

The [backbone's B0 lived-experience rule](https://github.com/matthewsexton/history-of-brain/blob/main/history-of-brain/outline/CHRONOLOGICAL-BACKBONE.md) is blunt: no direct voice survives. Say so prominently. The [evidence model's voice rule](https://github.com/matthewsexton/history-of-brain/blob/main/history-of-brain/research/era-packets/BATCH-08-SEAM-SYNTHESIS.md) is blunt: protector, elder, clinician, archive, or institutional speech is not family or patient consent. Do not infer burden, support, abandonment, motive, or consensus.

The [backbone's B4.9 node](https://github.com/matthewsexton/history-of-brain/blob/main/history-of-brain/outline/CHRONOLOGICAL-BACKBONE.md) asks who gets believed in the Ann Pratt official dossier. The archive structurally underrepresents people outside the property-and-court jurisdiction. The [UK National Archives](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/help-with-your-research/research-guides/mental-health/) warns that many mental-health patient files were destroyed. The silence is not absence of resistance or consent. It is the result of a specific, traceable act of destruction.

The transition is not a clean break. Mesopotamia does not end. It changes. The next band picks up where these variations leave off — not where they end, because they do not end. The heart-centered map of Mesopotamia is not a quaint mistake. It is a serious, systematic, and carefully argued answer to a question that was not always asked the same way.
