Antarctica has no indigenous population. There is no pre-colonial record of mental health in Antarctica. That is the honest answer. The records that exist are from the 20th century, from the researchers and workers who lived there. You can check them.

The Wellcome Collection has materials. The NLM has digitized editions. The British Museum has related materials. You can check them.

The shift from the other places to Antarctica is not a break. It is a different starting point. There is no pre-colonial record. There is only the 20th century. That is a different way of thinking about the mind.
