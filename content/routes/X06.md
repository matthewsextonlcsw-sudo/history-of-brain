## Draft

The political route is the story of how the mind became a political problem. Before the 19th century, mental illness was a local problem. The poor law dealt with it. The criminal law dealt with it. The family dealt with it. The state did not.

The 1845 Acts changed this. The Lunacy Commission was established. It was a political body. It reported to Parliament. It inspected asylums. It collected data. It made recommendations. The mind was now a matter of state policy.

The National Archives holds the records of this political process. There are local Acts of Parliament setting up individual asylums. There are Parliamentary Papers with reports, returns, and correspondence. There are Hansard records of parliamentary debates. You can search them online.

The Ministry of Health records (1798–2001) show the continuation of this political process. The MH series in the National Archives holds correspondence, reports, and administrative records. The KB series holds records of related organisations.

The Parliamentary Papers are a concrete example of the political route. They hold reports, returns, correspondence, and other records relating to individual asylums. You can access them online via Proquest UK Parliamentary Papers. The discussions in Parliament are in Hansard, which is available online on the Parliament website. The political route is documented in these papers: the debates, the reports, the returns.

The local Acts of Parliament are a concrete example of the political route. Each county that built an asylum needed a local Act. The Act named the asylum, the trustees, and the powers. You can find these Acts in the National Archives. They are the legal mechanism of the political route.

The political route is documented in the Parliamentary Papers, the Hansard records, and the local Acts of Parliament. The debates are in Hansard. The reports are in the Parliamentary Papers. The legal mechanism is in the local Acts. You can search them all online. The political route is real. The records show it.

The political route is not a story of good politics or bad politics. It is a story of how the state got involved in the mind. The involvement was not always benevolent. It was not always effective. But it was real. The records show it.
