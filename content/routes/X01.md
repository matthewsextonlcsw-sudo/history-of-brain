## Draft

The County Asylums Act of 1808 was the first step. The Lunacy Act of 1845 required every county in England to build an asylum. The records are in the National Archives today. You can check them.

The asylum was not a hospital. The asylum was a prison. The patient was not a person. The patient was a case. The record was not a story. The record was a file.

The Wellcome Collection has photographs. The British Museum has instruments. The National Archives has files. You can check them.

The shift from the medieval period to the 19th century is not a break. It is a continuation. The mind was still a body problem. The humors were still the explanation. But the scale was different. The asylum was the new scale.
