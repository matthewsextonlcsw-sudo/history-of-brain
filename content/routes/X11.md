## Draft

The advocacy route is the story of how the mind got a voice. The 20th century is the part that matters here. The records are in museums and archives today. You can check them.

The Wellcome Collection has texts. The British Museum has documents. The NLM has translations. You can check them.

The shift from the 19th century to the 20th century is not a break. It is a continuation. The mind was still a body problem. The humors were still the explanation. But the voice was the new scale.
