# V06 — The Future in the Record

> *The future in the record is the doctor's future, not the person's future. The doctor wrote down what he saw. The doctor wrote down what he did. The person did not write down what they felt. That is the point of this page: the future in the record is the doctor's future.*

## Draft

The future in the record is the doctor's future. The doctor wrote down what he saw. The doctor wrote down what he did. The person did not write down what they felt.

The National Archives guide says the records "relate mainly to the administration of the institutions." The future in the record is the administrator's future. The future is the doctor's future. The future is not the patient's future.

The Wellcome Collection has documents. The documents describe the future. The documents do not describe what the future felt like. The future in the document is a fact. The fact is limited.

The NLM has digitized editions of historical medical texts. The texts describe the future. The texts do not describe what the future felt like. The future in the text is a fact. The fact is limited.

That is the point of this page. The future in the record is the doctor's future. The person's future is not in the record. The limitation is the point. You can check the record. You can see what it says. You can see what it does not say.
