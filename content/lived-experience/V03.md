# V03 — The Body in the Record

> *The body in the record is the doctor's body, not the person's body. The doctor wrote down what he saw. The doctor wrote down what he did. The person did not write down what they felt. That is the point of this page: the body in the record is the doctor's body.*

## Draft

The body in the record is the doctor's body. The doctor wrote down what he saw. The doctor wrote down what he did. The person did not write down what they felt.

The National Archives has files. The files say what the doctor did. The files do not say what the person felt. The Wellcome Collection has photographs. The photographs show what the room looked like. The photographs do not show what the person felt.

That is the point of this page. The body in the record is the doctor's body. The person's body is not in the record. The limitation is the point. You can check the record. You can see what it says. You can see what it does not say.
