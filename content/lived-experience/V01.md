> *The record is a fact. The record is not a story. The record does not show what the person felt. The record does not show what the person thought. The record does not show what the person wanted. The record shows what the doctor wrote. That is the point of this page: the record is a fact, and the fact is limited.*

## Draft

The record is a fact. The record is not a story. The record does not show what the person felt. The record does not show what the person thought. The record does not show what the person wanted.

The doctor wrote down what he saw. He wrote down what he did. He did not write down what the person felt. The person did not write down what they felt. The record is the doctor's words, not the person's words.

The National Archives guide says it plainly: most patient files have been destroyed. The records that survive are administrative. The admission register gives you the name, the age, the date of admission, and the date of discharge or death. The quarterly return gives you the number of patients in the asylum. The correspondence with the poor law union gives you the name, the age, the type of disability, and whether the person was considered dangerous. None of these records give you what the person felt in the room.

Here is a concrete example. The register says a woman was admitted in 1852 and discharged in 1854. That is two years. The register does not say what those two years were like. It does not say whether she was afraid, or angry, or relieved. It does not say whether she wanted to leave. The two years are a fact. The feeling is not in the record.

The Wellcome Collection has photographs. The photographs show what the room looked like. The photographs do not show what the person felt. The photographs are a fact. The fact is limited.

The gap is not a flaw. It is a fact. The record is what it is. A good page names the gap and does not fill it with invention. It does not guess what the person felt. It does not imagine the person's inner life. It says: the record does not show this. That is the honest answer. And the honest answer is enough.

That is the point of this page: the record is a fact, and the fact is limited. The gap is part of the record. You can check the record. You can see what it says. You can see what it does not say.
