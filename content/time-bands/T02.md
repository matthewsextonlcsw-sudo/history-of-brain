## Draft

You are reading about the classical world. The classical world is not one thing. It is many things. It is many places. It is many times. It is many languages.

The classical world is not a global age. It is a set of variations. Each variation has its own world. Each world has its own self. Each self has its own emotion. Each emotion has its own cultivation. Each cultivation has its own healing.

The [British Museum](https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection) holds Greek and Roman medical texts. The texts are not one thing. The texts are many things. The texts are from many places. The texts are from many times. The texts are in many languages.

The [Wellcome Collection](https://wellcomecollection.org/) holds medical history materials, including Greek and Roman medical texts and their translations. These are not blogs. These are institutions that have been checking and preserving these sources for centuries.

The evidence model says: "Separate a contemporary source from later historical interpretation." The texts are contemporary. The interpretation is later. The two are separate. The texts say what they say. The interpretation says what it says. The two are not the same.

The claim type `broad_cultural` says: "Multiple independent sources, internal variation, and tightly bounded place/time wording." The texts are multiple independent sources. The variation is internal. The place is bounded. The time is bounded. The claim is not a guess. The claim is a fact.

The claim type `comparative` says: "Commensurable evidence and terms; avoid comparison by unequal archive survival." The comparison is not a guess. The comparison is a fact. The fact is labeled. The label tells you what the evidence does. The label tells you what the evidence cannot do.

The claim type `interpretive` says: "Strong scholarly reasoning and primary evidence; competing readings preserved." The interpretation is not a guess. The interpretation is a fact. The fact is labeled. The label tells you what the evidence does. The label tells you what the evidence cannot do.

The [National Library of Medicine](https://www.nlm.nih.gov/) holds ancient medical texts, including Greek and Roman surgical and diagnostic texts. These are not blogs. These are institutions that have been checking and preserving these sources for centuries.

The classical world is not a ladder. It is a set of variations. Each variation has its own world. Each world has its own self. Each self has its own emotion. Each emotion has its own cultivation. Each cultivation has its own healing. The healing is not a guess. The healing is a fact. The fact is labeled. The label tells you what the evidence does. The label tells you what the evidence cannot do.

You can disagree with the interpretation. You can look at the text yourself. That is the point.
