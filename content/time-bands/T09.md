This is not one world. It is a set of national and colonial variations, each with its own present, its own promise, its own violence. The dates overlap because the variations overlap. There is no single "present." There are many, in many places, in many jurisdictions.

The transition is not a clean break. The present does not end. It changes. The next band picks up where these variations leave off — not where they end, because they do not end.
