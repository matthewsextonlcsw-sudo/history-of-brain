## Draft

This is not one system. It is a set of national and colonial variations, each with its own institution, its own promise, its own violence. The dates overlap because the variations overlap. There is no single "institutional explosion." There are many, in many places, in many jurisdictions.

The [backbone's B7.1 node](https://github.com/matthewsexton/history-of-brain/blob/main/history-of-brain/outline/CHRONOLOGICAL-BACKBONE.md) names the asylum and the hospital: the institution is not one thing. It is a set of variations, each with its own architecture, its own promise, its own violence. The [backbone's B7.2 node](https://github.com/matthewsexton/history-of-brain/blob/main/history-of-brain/outline/CHRONOLOGICAL-BACKBONE.md) shows the institution and the community: the institution is not isolated. It is embedded in the community, and the community is embedded in the institution.

The [backbone's B7.3 node](https://github.com/matthewsexton/history-of-brain/blob/main/history-of-brain/outline/CHRONOLOGICAL-BACKBONE.md) shows the institution and the family: the family is the enforcement mechanism. The [R31 Japan packet](https://github.com/matthewsexton/history-of-brain/blob/main/history-of-brain/research/era-packets/BATCH-08-SEAM-SYNTHESIS.md) shows this in legal form: the 1950 law assigned duties to a legally designated protector, including financial and discharge-retrieval obligations. The family — or its legal proxy — was the enforcement mechanism. The [backbone's B7.4 node](https://github.com/matthewsexton/history-of-brain/blob/main/history-of-brain/outline/CHRONOLOGICAL-BACKBONE.md) shows the institution and the worker: the worker is the administration mechanism. The [R33 Aro packet](https://github.com/matthewsexton/history-of-brain/blob/main/history-of-brain/research/era-packets/BATCH-08-SEAM-SYNTHESIS.md) shows the worker's role in care: organizer/admin descriptions include "described nursing and medical roles" and "treatment and occupational activity." The workers were the care infrastructure.

The [backbone's B7.5 node](https://github.com/matthewsexton/history-of-brain/blob/main/history-of-brain/outline/CHRONOLOGICAL-BACKBONE.md) centers the patient: the patient is the subject. The [backbone's B7.6 node](https://github.com/matthewsexton/history-of-brain/blob/main/history-of-brain/outline/CHRONOLOGICAL-BACKBONE.md) shows the institution and the law: the law is the framework. The [backbone's B7.7 node](https://github.com/matthewsexton/history-of-brain/blob/main/history-of-brain/outline/CHRONOLOGICAL-BACKBONE.md) shows the institution and the drug: the drug is the mechanism.

The [UK National Archives](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/help-with-your-research/research-guides/mental-health/) warns that many mental-health patient files were destroyed. The silence is not absence of resistance or consent. It is the result of a specific, traceable act of destruction.

The transition is not a clean break. The institutional explosion does not end. It changes. The next band picks up where these variations leave off — not where they end, because they do not end.
