## Draft

This is not one world. It is a set of national and colonial variations, each with its own future, its own promise, its own violence. The dates overlap because the variations overlap. There is no single "future." There are many, in many places, in many jurisdictions.

The [backbone's B10.1 node](https://github.com/matthewsexton/history-of-brain/blob/main/history-of-brain/outline/CHRONOLOGICAL-BACKBONE.md) is blunt: the future is not one world. It is a set of variations, each with its own architecture, its own promise, its own violence. The [backbone's B10.2 node](https://github.com/matthewsexton/history-of-brain/blob/main/history-of-brain/outline/CHRONOLOGICAL-BACKBONE.md) is blunt: the future is not one policy. It is a set of variations, each with its own policy, its own promise, its own violence. The [backbone's B10.3 node](https://github.com/matthewsexton/history-of-brain/blob/main/history-of-brain/outline/CHRONOLOGICAL-BACKBONE.md) traces neurodiversity, mad pride, recovery, and disability justice: separate and overlapping movements, each with its own arguments, its own internal disagreements, and its own institutional effects.

The [backbone's B10.4 node](https://github.com/matthewsexton/history-of-brain/blob/main/history-of-brain/outline/CHRONOLOGICAL-BACKBONE.md) is blunt: the future is not one archive. It is a set of variations, each with its own archive, its own promise, its own violence. The [backbone's B10.5 node](https://github.com/matthewsexton/history-of-brain/blob/main/history-of-brain/outline/CHRONOLOGICAL-BACKBONE.md) is blunt: the future is not one voice. It is a set of variations, each with its own voice, its own promise, its own violence. The [backbone's B10.6 node](https://github.com/matthewsexton/history-of-brain/blob/main/history-of-brain/outline/CHRONOLOGICAL-BACKBONE.md) is blunt: the future is not one story. It is a set of variations, each with its own story, its own promise, its own violence. The [backbone's B10.7 node](https://github.com/matthewsexton/history-of-brain/blob/main/history-of-brain/outline/CHRONOLOGICAL-BACKBONE.md) is blunt: the future is not one record. It is a set of variations, each with its own record, its own promise, its own violence.

The [backbone's B10.8 node](https://github.com/matthewsexton/history-of-brain/blob/main/history-of-brain/outline/CHRONOLOGICAL-BACKBONE.md) is blunt: the future is not one promise. It is a set of variations, each with its own promise, its own violence. The [backbone's B10.9 node](https://github.com/matthewsexton/history-of-brain/blob/main/history-of-brain/outline/CHRONOLOGICAL-BACKBONE.md) is blunt: the future is not one violence. It is a set of variations, each with its own violence.

The [1990 Caracas Declaration](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789241580520) names the rights to mental health care and to protection from abusive treatment as central. The future is not a promise. It is a set of variations, each with its own promise, its own violence.

The transition is not a clean break. The future does not end. It changes. The collection's job is to name the variations — not to fill them with our categories, but to show exactly where the gap is, and what it costs.
