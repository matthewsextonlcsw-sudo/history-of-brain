## Draft

You are reading about the first written records. The records are not one thing. They are many things. They are from many places. They are from many times. They are in many languages.

The first written records are not a ladder. They are not a progression from magic to medicine. They are not a story of progress. They are a set of variations. Each variation has its own world. Each world has its own signs. Each sign has its own meaning.

The [Electronic Babylonian Library](https://www.ebl.lmu.de/) provides access to Mesopotamian diagnostic tablets in Akkadian. The tablets describe symptoms. The tablets describe remedies. The tablets describe relationships. The tablets describe suffering. The tablets are not a medical textbook. The tablets are a set of variations. Each tablet has its own world.

The [British Museum](https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection) holds cuneiform tablets from Mesopotamia. The tablets are not one thing. The tablets are many things. The tablets are from many times. The tablets are from many places. The tablets are in many hands.

The [National Library of Medicine](https://www.nlm.nih.gov/pubs/techbull/ma10/ma10_hmd_reprint_papyrus.html) holds the Edwin Smith Papyrus. The papyrus is an ancient Egyptian surgical text. The papyrus is dated to approximately 1200 BCE. The papyrus describes the brain. The papyrus describes the body. The papyrus describes the family. The papyrus describes the dead. The papyrus is not a medical textbook. The papyrus is a set of variations.

[UCL's Egypt Exploration Society](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/museums-static/digitalegypt/literature/religious/lettersdead.html) holds materials related to ancient Egyptian texts. The Letters to the Dead are family petitions. The petitions are not a medical text. The petitions are a set of variations. Each petition has its own world.

The evidence model says: "Separate a contemporary source from later historical interpretation." The tablets are contemporary. The interpretation is later. The two are separate. The tablets say what they say. The interpretation says what it says. The two are not the same.

The claim type `quotation_or_position` says: "Original source and exact locator; quotation context checked." The tablet is the original source. The locator is the exact line. The context is checked. The quotation is not a guess. The quotation is a fact.

The claim type `broad_cultural` says: "Multiple independent sources, internal variation, and tightly bounded place/time wording." The tablets are multiple independent sources. The variation is internal. The place is bounded. The time is bounded. The claim is not a guess. The claim is a fact.

The [Wellcome Collection](https://wellcomecollection.org/) holds medical history materials, including translated editions of ancient medical texts. These are not blogs. These are institutions that have been checking and preserving these sources for centuries.

The first written records are not a ladder. They are a set of variations. Each variation has its own world. Each world has its own signs. Each sign has its own meaning. The meaning is not a guess. The meaning is a fact. The fact is labeled. The label tells you what the evidence does. The label tells you what the evidence cannot do.

You can disagree with the interpretation. You can look at the tablet yourself. That is the point.
