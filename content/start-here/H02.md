## Draft

How to read the collection. The collection is a cited global history of the mind. The records are in museums and archives today. You can check them.

To read the collection, start with the Research section of each page. The Research section shows what the record says. Each fact has a source. You can check the source. You can see what the record says. You can see what the record does not say.

The Draft section is the story. The story shows what the record says about the topic. The story does not show what the record does not say. The limitation is the point.

The References section shows where the facts come from. Each reference has a URL. You can check the URL. You can see what the source says. You can see what the source does not say.

The National Archives guide says: "Lunatic asylums were first established in Britain in the mid-19th century." That is a fact. The fact is bounded. The fact is limited. The limitation is the point.

You can walk through the collection. You can look at the sources. You can disagree with the interpretation. That is the point.
