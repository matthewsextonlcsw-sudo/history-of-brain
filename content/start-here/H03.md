# H03 — The Source Rating Protocol

> *The source rating protocol is the foundation of this collection. It tells you what the source does. It tells you what the source cannot do. It tells you how to check the work. It tells you how to disagree with the work and still trust the source.*

## Draft

You are reading a collection. The collection is built on a set of rules. The rules are not a decoration. The rules are the foundation. Without the rules, the collection is a guess. With the rules, the collection is a claim with a trail. You can follow the trail. You can check the work. You can disagree with the work and still trust the source.

The rules say: "Never cite a source that has not been opened and checked." The rules say: "Use the strongest source appropriate to the claim; 'primary' does not automatically mean accurate, representative, ethical, or sufficient." The rules say: "Preserve disagreement, uncertainty, translation choices, archival silence, and source bias." The rules say: "No factual or interpretive claim becomes publishable because it sounds plausible."

The rules are not a list. The rules are a foundation. The foundation is the collection. The collection is the mind. The mind is the record. The record is the fact. The fact is the label. The label is the trail. The trail is the point.

You can walk through the collection. You can look at the sources. You can disagree with the interpretation. That is the point.
