## Draft

Welcome to the History of the Mind and Mental Health collection. This is a cited global history of the mind. The records are in museums and archives today. You can check them.

The collection is organized into seven zones: evidence, start-here, time-bands, lived-experience, guides, places, and routes. Each zone has its own purpose. Each zone has its own rules.

The evidence zone shows you how to read the evidence. The start-here zone shows you how to enter the collection. The time-band zone shows you what the record says about specific times. The lived-experience zone shows you what the record does and does not show. The guide zone shows you how to read each type of page. The place zone shows you what the record says about specific places. The route zone shows you what the record says about specific routes.

The National Archives guide says: "Lunatic asylums were first established in Britain in the mid-19th century." That is a fact. The fact is bounded. The fact is limited. The limitation is the point.

You can walk through the collection. You can look at the sources. You can disagree with the interpretation. That is the point.
