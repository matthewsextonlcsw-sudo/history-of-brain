## Draft

A start-here page is a door. The door shows how to enter the collection. It does not show what the record does not say.

A start-here page is not a deep page. It is a map of the collection. It tells you where the zones are and what each one does. It does not try to answer every question. It points you to the page that does.

The collection has seven zones: evidence, start-here, time-bands, lived-experience, guides, places, and routes. Each zone has a different job. The evidence zone shows you how to read the evidence. The time-band zone shows you what the record says about specific times. The lived-experience zone shows you whose voice survives. The places zone shows you what the record says about specific regions. The routes zone shows you how ideas and practices moved.

Start with the Research section. Each fact carries a source. You can open the source and check it. Then follow the links. A start-here page links out to the deeper pages. It does not pretend to be complete.

Here is how to pick a door. If you want to know what the record says about a time, go to the time-band zone. If you want to know whose voice survives, go to the lived-experience zone. If you want to know how an idea moved, go to the routes zone. If you want to know what the record says about a place, go to the places zone. The start-here page points you to the right door. It does not try to be every door at once. It is a map, not a destination. You pick the door that matches your question, and you walk through it.

That is how you read a start-here page: take the map, pick a door, and walk through it.
