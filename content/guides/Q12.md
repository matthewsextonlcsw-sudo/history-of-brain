# Q12 — How to Read a Lived-Experience Page

> *A lived-experience page is a window. The window shows what the record says about a specific experience. The window does not show what the record does not say. That is the point of this page: the lived-experience page is a window, and the window is limited.*

## Draft

A lived-experience page is a window. The window shows what the record says about a specific experience. The window does not show what the record does not say.

To read a lived-experience page, start with the Research section. The Research section shows what the record says. Each fact has a source. You can check the source. You can see what the record says. You can see what the record does not say.

The Draft section is the window. The window shows what the record says about the experience. The window does not show what the record does not say. The limitation is the point.

You can walk through the collection. You can look at the sources. You can disagree with the interpretation. That is the point.
