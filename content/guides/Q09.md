## Draft

A time-band page is a window. The window shows what the record says about a specific time. The window does not show what the record does not say.

To read a time-band page, start with the Research section. The Research section shows what the record says. Each fact has a source. You can check the source. You can see what the record says. You can see what the record does not say.

The Draft section is the window. The window shows what the record says about the time. The window does not show what the record does not say. The limitation is the point.

The National Archives guide says: "Lunatic asylums were first established in Britain in the mid-19th century." That is a fact. The fact is bounded. The fact is limited. The limitation is the point.

You can walk through the collection. You can look at the sources. You can disagree with the interpretation. That is the point.
